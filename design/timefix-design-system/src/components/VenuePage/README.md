VenuePage is the public page behind a workspace's own link (timefix.com/<handle>), in the shape guests know from Fresha and OpenTable: a photo grid as the cover, then a profile header (the logo, or its initials on a tile, over the photos' edge; the name; what kind of place it is; rating, open status and directions); the menu by category with Book on each; the team; about, opening hours and location; and a side card with the main action that stays in view. VenueCheckout is the booking steps after it (a breadcrumb, the choice on the left, a summary card with the total and the main button on the right); PersonTile picks a person or "Any professional".

**Use it for**: every module that books from a public link: services, tables and meetings now, and the modules that follow. **Don't use it for** a single page's own link (that is BookingFlow).

**The consumer provides**: `venue` (name, kind, rating, address, status, photos, about, hours), `items` with `categories`, `team`, and `onBook` / `onBookNow`, or an `aside` that replaces the side card's body (a table picker). On a phone the page is one column and the main button sits in a bar at the foot. `hours` is the week (Monday first, each day's open ranges in minutes), drawn by `OpeningHours`: a live pill ("Open · closes 8:00 PM", "Closed · opens tomorrow 10:00 AM") that checks again each minute and when the tab comes back, today's row picked out (`aria-current="date"`), each range on its own line, closed days quiet. `OpeningHours` and `openStatus` work on their own too (a link page, an embed).
