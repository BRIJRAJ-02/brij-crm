LinkEditor is where a person builds their link page: blocks on the left, a live phone preview on the right, and Publish once the draft differs from the live page.

**Use it for**: the link page editor. **Don't use it for** settings that take effect at once; this page drafts and publishes.

**The consumer provides**: `live` (the page guests see: profile, socials, blocks, look, accent, and `details`: site title, closing line, share image, search indexing), `onPublish(draft)`, a promise, and `booking` for the preview's booking block. The editor keeps the draft itself.

**States**: the profile row (photo with crop, name, title, bio, photo shape), Design (template, font from the guest looks' list with each name drawn in its face, own colour, cover and background photos, each cropped) and Details beside each other, Import ("Paste the page": on a Linktree, Beacons or Bento page press ⌘A then ⌘C and paste; the clipboard's HTML gives each link its address and words, or paste the HTML or a list of links; links are deduped and the page's own links dropped, then reviewed with ticks and added at the end as buttons. The address field stays: type one and an info Callout says plainly that the real app opens it, with "Paste the page instead"; nothing here fetches another site), blocks with a grip, a tile, title and address, click counts and a show switch (hidden blocks dim). A deleted block leaves "Deleted · Undo". A block's panel is calm first: a link shows only its title and address. Every block but dividers and social icons has one More options fold, grouped under small headings: Look (a link's line under the title, Shown as with its picture, and icon) and When and who (show from and until, where a row says "Not showing today" outside them; size, small sitting two to a row; and for links and media Ask first: sensitive, 18+, 21+). Whatever is set there shows as small chips above the fold ("Featured card", "Shows 1 Dec to 5 Dec", "18 and over"); pressing one opens it. Archive takes a block off the page and keeps it in Archived, where Restore brings it back and Delete asks first. New kinds: Next times, Video or music, Picture, Gallery, Banner, and a Header that folds shut. PublishBar counts unpublished changes, then publishes and says so.

**Interaction**: drag a grip, or focus it and press Space, arrows and Space (Esc puts it back), to reorder (lib/drag.ts, one feel with the board): the copy lifts under the pointer, a dashed gap opens where it will land and the other blocks slide aside. A click on a row (or Tab to it and Enter) opens it in the side panel; the list and panel slide side by side (8px. Typing updates the preview at once. New blocks drop in at the top and open for editing; an edited row gets a brief accent wash. Pasting a social link swaps in the network's mark.

**Replaces**: the link page editor pieces: block rows, LinkListRowOrder, the phone preview and their dialogs.
