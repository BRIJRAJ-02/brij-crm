HostedForm is the form a guest fills in, on its hosted page, inside an embed and in the builder's preview, one tree for all three.

**Use it for**: any Timefix form a visitor answers, one question at a time (`mode="one"`, Typeform's way) or every question on one card (`mode="page"`, Tally's). **Don't use it for** forms inside the app: use Form and its fields at 36px.

**The consumer provides**: `title`, `description`, `questions` (each `{ id, kind, title, description?, required?, placeholder?, options? }` across the kinds in `QUESTION_GROUPS`: text, choices, scores, dates and times, files and agreement, layout and data), `onSubmit(answers)` (a rejection shows the alert and keeps the answers), `onFinishLater(answers)` resolving with a link, `booking` (open days and times for the booking step), `onUpload(file, onProgress)`, `thanks` (title, line, and an optional `action` button that opens its https link in a new tab), `onReach(questionId)` called once per question a visitor reaches one at a time (it feeds the owner's "Where people stop"), `submitLabel`, `prefill`, `brand`, `logic`. `QUESTION_KINDS` names and icons every kind; `checkAnswer` and `answerText` are the one checker and the one reader the builder shares. `look` (a GuestLookValue) dresses the form: its ground, buttons and choices in the accent, its face, light or dark.

**Steps, endings, score, language, corners, editing in place**: `mode="steps"` shows a page per group of questions (a new group starts at each question with `step`, `stepsOf` groups them), with "Step 2 of 3", the step's title, Back and Next. `endings` are other thank-you messages: a jump to `end:<id>`, or `endingFor(answers, score)` (the form's routes), ends there; `{{score}}` in their words shows the score (`scoreOf`). `onSubmit` gets a second argument `{ ending, score, secs }`. `lang` (`FORM_LANGS`) translates the form's own words (OK, Submit, Next, Back, the step line, the default thank you); the owner's questions and the answer checks stay as written. `corners="square"` steps every radius down. `onEdit` turns the headline, the line under it and the last button into in-place inputs for the builder's preview. On one page `onReach` fires when a question first gets an answer, on steps when its step shows, so drop-off works for every layout. Questions can carry `earliest`/`latest` (dates, `'today'` or a day), `fewest`/`most` (multiple choice) and `other` (an Other card with a line to type; it sends "Other: …").

**Per-question logic**: besides the form's `logic`, a question's own `jumps` (by answer, to a later question, the end or an ending; `is: '*'` is the Otherwise line) steer one-at-a-time, and a question with `showIf` is skipped (one at a time) or left out (all on one page) until the earlier answer matches; skipped answers aren't sent.

**States**: answering, invalid (red line under the control, the control shakes), sending (the button keeps its width with a spinner), failed, saved for later, thank you.

**Interaction**: Enter goes on (Shift and Enter breaks a long answer's line); a letter key picks a choice card; a single choice, a dropdown pick or a rating goes on by itself 450ms later, never from the last question; Back returns. Steps slide side by side (transitions.dev 08, 8px, 250ms) in a card that eases to each step's height (01). A refused step shakes (12). Choice ticks draw (25); stars pop on the bounce curve; the dropdown and date open in the shared Popover (05). Finish later opens its Saved row as an accordion (21) and Copy link swaps to Copied. The thank you draws its check (10) and reveals its words, staggered 40ms (18), over the sent answers.

**Replaces**: HostedForm, HostedFormSteps, Question, AddressQuestion, FileQuestion, RankingQuestion, SignatureQuestion, FinishLater, FormBooking, BookingView, SentAnswers, FormParts.
