WeekCalendar is the team week: who is busy when, and where a new meeting can go.

**Use it for**: the Calendar page's week and day views (pass one day for a day view), a team member's week, and picking a time for a one-off meeting. **Don't use it for** a month overview or a guest picking a slot (BookingFlow).

**The consumer provides**: `days` (weekday, date, a full label, `isToday`), `events` (day column, start and end minutes, `kind`: meeting, internal, in-person or calendar, guests, location, `isLocked` for one that opens but never moves), `busy` blocks from other calendars, `startHour` and `endHour`, `now`, `hour12`, and `onSelect`, `onJoin`, `onReschedule`, `onEventChange(event, { day, start, end })` (makes events movable; called once on drop), and `eventDetails(event, close)`: what an event's popover shows under its title and time in place of Join and Reschedule (a block's Repeats and Remove, a calendar event's About this event); return undefined for the default.

**States**: Timefix meetings are soft blue, internal time grey, in-person orange, a connected calendar's own event hatched like busy time with a hairline edge; a 30 minute event is one line. Time held by other calendars is hatched, never filled. An event that overlaps anything carries a small Conflict tag. Today's header is blue and an accent now-line with a dot crosses today.

**Interaction**: click an event for the shared Popover with time, place, guests, Join and Reschedule (menu dropdown motion: 250ms in from 0.97, 150ms out); the event stays outlined while it is open. With `onEventChange`, drag an event to move it in 15 minute steps and across days, or drag its bottom edge to change its length: the source stays as a 40% ghost, the moved copy lifts (shadow-md, 1.02) and shows its new range, a busy overlap shows the Conflict tag live, Esc puts it back, and the drop settles over 250ms. By keyboard: focus an event, Enter picks it up, arrows move it 15 minutes or a day, Shift with arrows changes its length, Enter drops, Esc cancels (Space opens its details); every step is announced. Drag on empty space to select a range in 15 minute steps; the range shows its times. By keyboard: Tab to a day, Enter places a 30 minute range at 9:00, arrows move it, Shift with arrows changes its length, Esc clears. Selections are announced.

**Replaces**: WeekCalendar, TeamWeek, TeamDay, MatchCalendar.
