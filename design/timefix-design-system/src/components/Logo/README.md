The Timefix logo: the mark (an ink circle whose tick breaks out of its top right) and the lowercase "timefix" wordmark.

**Use it for**: the sidebar's workspace switcher when no workspace logo is set, sign in and onboarding, the "Powered by Timefix" foot on public pages, emails. **Don't** recolour it, add effects, rebuild the wordmark in a font, or place the mark on a coloured fill.

**The consumer provides**: `size` (the mark's height in px; the wordmark follows at 0.72 of it).

**Themes**: the mark's circle takes `text` and its tick `surface`, so it is ink on light and white on dark, matching the brand's `timefix-mark.svg` and `timefix-mark-on-dark.svg`. The wordmark swaps between the brand's light and dark files.

**Clear space**: keep at least half the mark's size empty around the lockup. Smallest sizes: mark 16px, lockup 20px.

**Source**: `apps/web/public/brand/` on main (the same files the app's sign in and booking pages use).
