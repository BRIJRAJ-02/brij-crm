QuestionList is the form builder's Questions tab: the form's questions as cards; a question opens in the list's place, so the page keeps one editor column beside its one preview.

**Use it for**: building a form, a booking page's extra questions, any ordered list of questions a visitor answers. **Don't use it for** the answers themselves (HostedForm) or for reordering plain options (the settings' Choices list does that).

**The consumer provides**: `QuestionBuilder` takes `questions` and `onChange(questions)` and owns selection, duplicate, delete with Undo, and add. The parts stand alone: `QuestionList` (`questions`, `selectedId`, `onSelect`, `onReorder`, `onRequiredChange`, `onDuplicate`, `onDelete`, `onAdd`, `freshIds`, `notice`), `QuestionSettings` (`question`, `onChange`, optional `onClose`, `openSection` to open More options at a group), `questionChips`, `QuestionTypePicker` (`onPick`) and `QuestionTypeTile`. Types, names and icons come from HostedForm's `QUESTION_KINDS`, so the builder and the guest form never disagree.

**Calm settings**: a question's settings show only the essentials: its type, its words, the choices for choice types (with Pick one, Pick several or Dropdown), what consent, a checkbox or a hidden field needs, and Required. Everything else folds under one More options, in groups under small headings: **Answer rules** (Add "Other"; Fewest and Most picks; a date's Earliest and Latest day; a number's or scale's Lowest and Highest; a scale's end words), **Logic and points** (Show only if an earlier choice question's answer, where hidden answers aren't kept; Start a new step here with an optional step title; After this question per answer: next, a later question, the thank you or another ending, with an Otherwise line once any answer jumps; Give points per choice, the total is the score; and any broken-rule warning), **Saves to** (with `saveTargets`, Save answer to a record field, "Kept with the submission only" by default, and Replace what's on the record; and for choice questions Use as "How did you hear about us" in Insights), and **Help text** (description and placeholder). Each rule that is set shows as a small chip (`questionChips`) under the card's title and above the fold: Required, "From today", "2 to 4 picks", "With Other", a purple "Shown if …", "1 jump", "Points", "Saves to Person › Job title", "In Insights", "Help text", and an orange chip for each broken rule (`logicProblems`: a deleted question or choice, an ending gone, a jump backwards). Pressing a chip (a Tag with `onPress`) opens More options at its group, scrolls it into view and focuses its heading; on a card it opens that question first. The card's grey line is just its type and how many choices.

**Steps, presets and filters**: cards show "Step 2 · About your company" above a question that starts a step; step lines carry a ⋯ menu: Rename step (a Dialog) and Merge with the step above, and the card menu toggles a step. The type list's Ready-made group (`presets`, QUESTION_PRESETS by default) adds a filled-in question: How did you hear about us, Company size, Job title, Phone number, Budget with points. A Logic filter (All · Logic n, or "n to check") shows only questions with rules. Jumps list `endings` as destinations.

**States**: empty list, a question calm (More options shut, chips for what is set), More options open at a group, cards at rest, hovered, selected (accent edge and ring), dragging (the card lifts under the pointer, a dashed gap where it will land, the others slide aside), a fresh card dropping in, "Deleted · Undo", a question open ("All questions" back, "2 of 5").

**Interaction**: press a card (or Tab to it and Enter) to open it in place; it slides in from the side (08) and "All questions" goes back. Adding or duplicating a question opens it. Drag a card by its grip (lib/drag.ts, the same feel as the board: the copy follows the pointer, the others slide aside over 250ms, the drop glides in), or focus the grip, Space, arrows and Space to drop, Esc to put it back; each step is announced. Inside the Logic filter a card moves next to the shown card it lands beside. New and duplicated cards drop in at their place (rows 0fr to 1fr, 12px rise). Delete collapses into an Undo strip (21) for six seconds. "Add question" opens the type picker in the shared Popover (05), about 640px wide from the button's right edge and never taller than the window: a search pinned on top (it keeps focus, its ring unclipped), a compact list (32px rows: a 24px icon well and the label) grouped Ready-made, Text, Choices, Date & time, Rating & scale, Files, Layout, and beside it a live preview of the active type drawn by the guest form's own QuestionInput, with its one-line description and what it fills ("Fills Person › Email", "Counted in Insights"). Typing filters at once ("No type matches" with Show every type); ↑ ↓ move and the preview follows (the search names the active row as its active descendant); Enter or a click adds; Esc closes. Ready-made questions each carry their own mark (megaphone, building, briefcase, phone, wallet) on an accent well. The new question rises into the list and opens for editing.

**Replaces**: QuestionList, the question editor's shell, the form's OptionsEditor use and the question type menu.
