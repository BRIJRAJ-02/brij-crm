QuestionList is the form builder's Questions tab: the form's questions as cards; a question opens in the list's place, so the page keeps one editor column beside its one preview.

**Use it for**: building a form, a booking page's extra questions, any ordered list of questions a visitor answers. **Don't use it for** the answers themselves (HostedForm) or for reordering plain options (the settings' Choices list does that).

**The consumer provides**: `QuestionBuilder` takes `questions` and `onChange(questions)` and owns selection, duplicate, delete with Undo, and add. The parts stand alone: `QuestionList` (`questions`, `selectedId`, `onSelect`, `onReorder`, `onRequiredChange`, `onDuplicate`, `onDelete`, `onAdd`, `freshIds`, `notice`), `QuestionSettings` (`question`, `onChange`, optional `onClose`), `QuestionTypePicker` (`onPick`) and `QuestionTypeTile`. Types, names and icons come from HostedForm's `QUESTION_KINDS`, so the builder and the guest form never disagree.

**Logic, points and presets**: each question's settings fold "Logic and points" under More options: Show only if (an earlier choice question's answer; hidden answers aren't kept), Start a new step here with an optional step title (cards show "Step 2 · About your company" above it, and the card menu toggles it), After this question (per answer: next, another later question, or after they send), and Give points (per choice, the total is the score). Choice questions can be marked "Use as How did you hear about us in Insights". With `saveTargets`, Save answer to picks the record field an answer fills ("Kept with the submission only" by default). The type list's Ready-made group (`presets`, QUESTION_PRESETS by default) adds a filled-in question: How did you hear about us, Company size, Job title, Phone number, Budget with points. Each card's grey line names what it carries: jumps, Points, In Insights, Saves to…; a purple "Shown if …" chip and an orange chip for each broken rule (`logicProblems`: a deleted question or choice, an ending gone, a jump backwards) sit under it, and the settings repeat the warning. A Logic filter (All · Logic n, or "n to check") shows only questions with rules. Step lines carry a ⋯ menu: Rename step (a Dialog) and Merge with the step above. Jumps list `endings` as destinations and add an Otherwise line once any answer jumps. Type settings: choice questions switch between Pick one, Pick several and Dropdown and can Add "Other"; multiple choice takes Fewest and Most picks; dates take an Earliest (any, today, a day) and Latest day; consent says its record is kept; Save answer to adds "Replace what's on the record".

**States**: empty list, cards at rest, hovered, selected (accent edge and ring), dragging (faint ghost, lifted preview, dashed slot), a fresh card dropping in, "Deleted · Undo", a question open ("All questions" back, "2 of 5").

**Interaction**: press a card (or arrow to it and Space) to open it in place; it slides in from the side (08) and "All questions" goes back. Adding or duplicating a question opens it. Drag a card by its grip, or focus the grip, Enter, arrows and Enter to drop. New and duplicated cards drop in at their place (rows 0fr to 1fr, 12px rise). Delete collapses into an Undo strip (21) for six seconds. "Add question" opens the type picker in the shared Popover (05), about 640px wide from the button's right edge and never taller than the window: a search pinned on top (it keeps focus, its ring unclipped), a compact list (32px rows: a 24px icon well and the label) grouped Ready-made, Text, Choices, Date & time, Rating & scale, Files, Layout, and beside it a live preview of the active type drawn by the guest form's own QuestionInput, with its one-line description and what it fills ("Fills Person › Email", "Counted in Insights"). Typing filters at once ("No type matches" with Show every type); ↑ ↓ move and the preview follows (the search names the active row as its active descendant); Enter or a click adds; Esc closes. Ready-made questions each carry their own mark (megaphone, building, briefcase, phone, wallet) on an accent well. The new question rises into the list and opens for editing.

**Replaces**: QuestionList, the question editor's shell, the form's OptionsEditor use and the question type menu.
