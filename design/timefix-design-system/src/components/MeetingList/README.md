MeetingList is the Meetings page: every booked meeting grouped by day, with what is next easy to see and act on.

**Use it for**: the Meetings list, a contact's meetings, and (as MeetingCard) a meeting on a dashboard or a record page. **Don't use it for** finding free time (WeekCalendar).

**The consumer provides**: `meetings` sorted by start (time, title, invitee, guests, place, status, and `host` when the list shows a team's), `now`, `hour12`, `loading`, the handlers `onOpen` (opens the meeting's drawer; makes rows focusable), `onReschedule`, `onCancel`, `onUndoCancel`, `onCancelSettled` (the undo window passed, email the guest now; `undoMs`, 8 seconds by default), `onCopyLink`, `onNoShow`, `onNote`, `onConfirm` and `onDecline` (requests show Confirm and Decline in place of the hover actions), `requests` (a first "Needs your OK" group in the same row style, each row showing its day), `isFullWidth` (kept for older callers; the list always fills its container now), `highlightId` (a row that just arrived), `conflictIds` (rows that clash with busy time get a small "Clash" tag) and an `emptyAction`. MeetingCard takes one `meeting` and an optional `strip` (tone, text, action).

**Calendar events and notes**: a meeting with `kind: 'calendar'` is an event from a connected calendar, not a booking: its second line is the calendar's name with a calendar icon, a grey Calendar tag sits in the status, and it has no row actions (pressing it still calls `onOpen`, to read it). `note` puts a short grey line in the status, such as a request's "Declines in 19 hr".

**States**: three levels: a day heading with a count, one card per day, rows inside it split by hairlines; "Needs your OK" gets an accent edge. A row reads its start in ink with its length under it (a request leads with its day; under 640px its day and time take their own line over the meeting, and on a phone the note and Decline / Confirm share the last line), then the meeting's name over who and where. Day headers ("Wednesday 11 November", `formatLongDay`) stick while their day scrolls, with a Today, Tomorrow or Yesterday chip and a count. MeetingCard reads "Wed 11 Nov · 1:00 – 1:30 PM" (`formatDay` and `formatClockRange`). A blue now-line with a dot at each end sits between what is over and what is next; past rows go grey. Statuses are tags with shapes (Confirmed, Pending, Cancelled, No-show, Completed). Loading shows skeleton rows; empty says what will appear and offers one action.

**Interaction**: with `onOpen`, a row opens on press, or on Enter or Space once Tab has focused it; its own buttons keep their meaning. Hovering or focusing a row washes it and slides in Reschedule, Cancel and a more menu (the shared Menu: the trigger stays pressed while open; destructive last, under a separator). Cancel acts at once: the row folds to 44px and resolves into "Cancelled · Undo" in place; the guest is emailed only after the undo window, when it reads "Cancelled · Guest emailed". The `highlightId` row drops in under its day (0 to full height with a 12px rise, 250ms) and keeps an accent wash for a moment. Data arriving cross-fades from the skeleton over 400ms (skeleton reveal); the empty text reveals with a stagger. MeetingCard's strip action nudges its arrow on hover.

**Replaces**: MeetingCard, BookingList.
