BookingsFlow is the whole Bookings module as one live, full-screen prototype over a dummy team: Meetings, Pages, Availability and Routing, plus the guest's side, built only from the system's components.

**Use it for**: reviewing every booking feature end to end, and as the map for building the real module. **Don't use it for** one screen; compose the parts it uses.

**The consumer provides**: `initialState` (`NORTHWIND`, the default, rebuilt relative to today; or `FIRST_VISIT`) and `showDemoMenu`. Wrap it in a `ToastProvider`; the root fills its parent's height. State is one reducer (`bookingsReducer`, typed `BookingsAction`s) in `state.ts`; the seed is `seed.ts`; screens live in `meetings.tsx`, `drawer.tsx`, `pages.tsx`, `editor.tsx`, `create.tsx`, `share.tsx`, `availability.tsx`, `routing.tsx`, `guests.tsx` and `guest.tsx`.

**How to walk it**: Meetings opens on Northwind: List / Week / Team day, Mine / Everyone / a person, Upcoming / Past / Cancelled, "Needs your OK" with Confirm and Decline, a clash card (See conflict opens Week at that hour), New meeting, and drag on empty time in Week or Team day to block, hold or book. Open a row for the drawer (Join, Reschedule, Cancel with Undo, Reassign, no-show, notes, Link to record). Pages has Mine / Team rows with Share (Offer specific times, One-time links, Embed) and the editor (stats, Basics, When, Who with round robin, Always joins (teammates on every meeting while the hosts rotate; times need them free too, and the meeting shows "with" them) and Manage groups, Questions, After booking, Look). A restaurant's table page can give Lunch and Dinner their own pacing and last seating (More options), and Reservations shows each shift's load as a strip of 15-minute bars against its cap. Availability holds saved hours, time off, calendars and the team week. Routing tests rules. A salon or restaurant adds Clients (Guests at a restaurant): everyone who booked, gathered by email or phone, A to Z with search and All / Coming up / Regulars / Not back in 60 days; a row opens their profile (visits, spend or usual party, no-shows, last visit; phone and email to copy; tags, notes, birthday; a restaurant's allergies, shown on every reservation; a salon's patch test and colour formulas, with a warning before colour when the test is missing or over six months old). It also opens from a reservation's menu (Guest profile) and a salon booking's drawer (Client record). The Demo menu starts fresh (brief steps 1 to 3: the Create sheet), plays the edge cases, and the guest scenarios (step 6; closing one plays step 7 on the host side).

**Interaction**: keyboard throughout (Tab, arrows, Enter, Esc; ⌘↵ creates, ⌘S saves). Motion: modal, page side-by-side, card resize, accordion, text morph and soft swap, rows dropping in with a wash, Undo toasts.

**Replaces**: BookingsScreen, BookingsTabs, BookingsTopBar, PageEditor, NewPageDialog, LinksScreen, SharingPanel, ShareTimesCard, EmbedCard, OneOffLinksCard, AvailabilityScreen, TeamWeekScreen, TeamDayView.
