# Roadmap (20 October 2026)

The owner asked for a plan covering a multi user tenant, the sidebar, more than one calendar per person, more features around forms, availability and booking, and every pending item. Four read only research passes (scheduling, forms, tenancy and shell, the whole CRM scope) were checked against the code at 80d84ec4 and references from cal.com, Calendly, SavvyCal, Chili Piper, HubSpot, Typeform, Tally, Fillout, Attio, Linear and timefix. [waves-2026-10-20.md](waves-2026-10-20.md) holds the lanes running now.

## Owner decisions (20 October 2026)

- After wave 1: CRM team basics and scheduling with forms get equal capacity.
- Sidebar: a Scheduling group (Bookings, Availability, Booking pages, Forms) replaces Captures; Inbox and Tasks on top, Favourites, Records, Lists, then Trash, Invite, Settings and Help at the foot.
- Calendars: the Calendly style design (up to 6 accounts, every calendar with conflict and show switches and a colour, one destination with a per page override, several schedules per person) plus the Outlook adapter in the same release, so Outlook moves from release 2 into release 1.
- Billing: a merchant of record, Paddle or Lemon Squeezy, chosen at the billing spec.

## Where we stand

About 11 of the 59 scope features are done, 24 partly and 24 not started. The biggest gap is that a team can't use it yet: there are no invites, no workspace switching and no import. Scheduling and forms are ahead of the CRM core.

Bugs found by the research:
- Booking events go to the member's oldest calendar connection, not the default (`core/booking/connected.ts`, spec 0027 AC-711). Being fixed in the calendar sync lane.
- One failing calendar account closes a whole booking page. Being fixed in the same lane.
- A collective page ignores a co-host's bookings on other pages, so it can double book. Fixed with round robin (spec 0029).

## Release 2: sellable

Plans and limits with seats; billing (checkout, upgrade, downgrade); templates and onboarding; audit log and the privacy and terms pages; full export and a rehearsed restore; an operator console; sessions and two factor; the access rules admin; files on R2; scale hardening to a million records; booking embeds (inline, popup, floating button); Outlook; routing from a form to a booking page or person; round robin; the team calendar; forms analytics, consent and retention.

Done when: checkout to upgrade to limits works end to end in test mode, a restore is rehearsed, a full security review passes, the million record run passes, the main flows pass a WCAG AA audit, the legal pages are live and Sentry alerts are wired.

## Release 3: competitive

Lists and pipelines; reports and dashboards; automations; the public API and webhooks; sending email from records (Gmail send first, sync after Google's security assessment); duplicate detection and merge; computed attributes, layouts and the schema map; booking no shows, analytics and a meeting inbox; Zoom and Teams locations; forms popups, partial submissions, scoring, templates and themes.

## Release 4: differentiated

Shared notes with live editing, sequences, enrichment, Slack and Zapier, connectors, the AI assistant and AI attributes; Apple calendars, payments, group seats and recurring bookings.

## Decisions taken as recommended unless the owner says otherwise

- Sign up stays closed except for an invited email, until billing opens it.
- Owners and admins invite; a fourth role, guest, reads only what's shared and takes no seat.
- A removed member's booking pages pause, their calendars disconnect, and their records and meetings stay with reassignment offered.
- Deleting a workspace is owner only, confirmed by typing its address, soft deleted with a 30 day purge.
- Joining by email domain is off by default; when on, an admin approves requests.
- Sidebar preferences are stored on the server per member, so they follow the person.
- Only each account's main calendar checks for conflicts until the person turns others on; a failing calendar is skipped with a warning.
- Private events show as Busy to everyone but their owner.
- Forms keep Classic as the default type; steps and conditions use a simple "show when", no flow canvas.
- Form notifications email the owner per submission; a digest is opt in.
- Analytics without cookies come first; visitor tracking later.
- Postgres search first; a search engine only if the million record test misses its budget.
- Payments, group seats, recurring bookings and Apple calendars wait.
