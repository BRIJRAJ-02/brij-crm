# Work waves (from 20 October 2026)

The owner asked to plan everything in parallel and make sure what was built is complete. This is the plan, based on a read only coverage audit of landing2 at ef6efa82 (polish items F1 to F40, specs 0019 to 0028, and the scope). [polish-phases.md](polish-phases.md) still holds the owner's feedback as given.

## Rules every lane follows

- One worktree per lane, inside the repo under `.claude/worktrees/`, on a `wip/*` branch. Never in a temporary folder: a restart on 20 October wiped every lane kept in one.
- Commit after each green step. Never push; the main thread reviews, merges into landing2, tests and pushes.
- The house skills for the area, the full design engineer set for screens, Mobbin references for layout and interaction, and the reviewers for the area before handing back.
- Owner decisions in force: field edges are #eaeaea (light) and #303237 (dark); nothing shrinks on press; Google, the calendar providers and the five social networks are the only brand marks; the public booking page `/b/:key` is frozen; the public form page `/f/:key` is open for its redesign.
- Each merge into landing2 is followed by `pnpm check`, the suites, the stories in Chromium and WebKit, and `pnpm size`. The first load is at 246 of its 250 kB budget, so a lane that adds to it must split lazily.
