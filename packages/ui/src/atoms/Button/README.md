# Button

The one button: 26px tall with a 13px medium label, in five variants, built on React Aria so it works the same by mouse, touch and keyboard. This folder also holds `ToggleButton` (a button that stays pressed), `SplitButton` (a main action with a menu of its variants), `ButtonGroup` (a few buttons joined into one control) `CopyButton` (copies a value and says so in place) and `ButtonLink` (a link drawn as a Button, for an action that goes somewhere: Join the call, Add to Google Calendar, the .ics download on a booking's page). `SplitButton` (SplitButton.tsx) and the cut label tooltip (CutButton.tsx, loaded on demand) sit in files of their own, so the menu and overlay code they need stays out of the first load, which draws only plain Buttons; SplitButton draws its halves with `SplitFrame` and `SplitHalf` from Button.tsx.

## Why it exists

Ported from the design system artifact's Button card. Every pressable control in the app is one of these; a second button style is how a design system dies, so new looks are variants here.

## Use

```tsx
<Button icon="settings" onPress={openSettings}>View settings</Button>
<Button variant="primary" kbd="⌘↵" onPress={save}>Add to list</Button>
<Button slot="close">Cancel</Button> {/* in a Modal's footer: ESC by default */}
<Button variant="primary" isPending>Saving</Button>
<Button icon="ellipsis" label="More actions" onPress={openMenu} />
<Button variant="primary" icon="plus" label="New form" onPress={create}>New</Button>
<ToggleButton isSelected={showArchived} onChange={setShowArchived}>Show archived</ToggleButton>
<SplitButton onPress={save} menu={<Menu label="Save options">…</Menu>}>Save</SplitButton>
<CopyButton value={apiKey} label="Copy the key" />
```

- `CopyButton` takes `isRound` (a pill: a link page's address under the person's name) and `name`, a fuller accessible name while it hasn't copied, starting with its visible label, for a label that is the value itself ("timefix.com/ada, copy link").
- **One primary per surface.** `primary` goes on the action that commits: Save, Add to list, Select record. Pair it with its shortcut (`kbd="⌘↵"`); in a Modal's footer a submit gets it by default.
- `secondary` (the default) for view controls (View settings, Import / Export) and Cancel (in a Modal's footer it shows `ESC` by itself).
- `danger` for a control that starts a destructive action, such as Delete in the bulk bar or Archive in settings: red label and edge on the raised surface, so it holds 4.5:1 in both themes.
- `danger-solid` for the final commit that destroys, such as Delete or Archive in a confirm dialog: a `danger-fill` with an `on-danger` label (5.3:1 light, 5.4:1 dark). One per dialog, in place of the primary.
- `ghost` for undo style actions that shouldn't compete, such as Discard changes, and for icon buttons inside dense rows.
- `value` for a value that opens something, in a list of values (a member's Scheduling access in their details, 6 November 2026): its text with a chevron, no box, its first letter on the plain values' edge, as Select's inline variant draws. Why not `ghost`: its padding stood the value 8px right of every other one in the list.
- `link` for a quiet text action at label size with no box, the control's height kept for its hit area: a card footer's Sign out or Back (first run, spec 0036). A variant rather than `ghost`, whose body sized label read as a large text button beside the footer's small "Signed in as" line (owner feedback, 21 October 2026).
- `ghost-danger` for a quiet icon button that deletes at once and offers Undo (a form question's Delete): ghost at rest, the danger colour under the pointer and on keyboard focus. A variant rather than `danger`, whose red border at rest is too loud in a row of quiet icons.
- `warning` for trouble that asks for a look, never for an action that changes anything: a Team day's "2 issues", which opens their list (31 October 2026, the owner asked for warnings in a warning colour). The orange tag's fill with its words, AA on each other in both themes; under the pointer its edge darkens. A variant rather than `ghost`, whose grey label read as one more view control beside Day, Week and Filter.
- `field` for a picker that stands in a stack of Fields: a form question's answer type, above its Question and Placeholder (the owner, 5 November 2026: "the field type inside"). It draws a Field's box exactly (`size-control` tall, the prototype's filled input since spec 0072's tier 2: `surface-subtle` with no edge at rest, `surface-hover` under the pointer, `radius-md`, `space-12` in, the value in body type), fills its row, and puts `fieldName` ("Type") inside at the start, at the width and with the hairline divider of a Field's `prefixWidth="label"` name, so its icon starts on the line where the Fields' values start; the value, then `iconRight` (`chevrons-up-down`) at the end. The whole box is the trigger. Open, it wears a typed in Field's white fill, accent edge and `accent-border` ring; disabled, a read only Field's fill with no edge, at full strength (leave the chevrons off). Why a variant and not a Select or a new part: the trigger opens the grouped type Menu that Add a question opens too, so it must be a MenuTrigger's Button, and a second pressable component would copy Button's states. Stories: FieldVariant, FieldFocused, FieldDisabled.
- `dashed` only for "add" affordances that grow a set: Filter, Add sort, and "+ Custom" after a booking page's length chips (ChoiceChips `end`), which adds a choice rather than being one (22 October 2026).
- `ButtonGroup` (no longer used by LinkList, which shows quiet ghost actions since the owner asked on 20 October 2026) joins a few secondary icon Buttons that act on one thing into one bordered control, sharing edges as SplitButton's halves do: a list row's Open, Copy link and More (LinkList). It takes Buttons, a CopyButton, or a MenuTrigger with its Button, each with its own `label`; `label` names the group for screen readers. Why not SplitButton: that is one action and its variants, this is several actions of equal weight. Added 20 October 2026 in place of LinkList's own joined box. A Button with `isExpanded` (and `controls`) is a disclosure: it opens and closes a region, reads as expanded or collapsed, and draws held down while open, as a ToggleButton does; a danger one keeps its red on a faint red tint (a guest's Cancel booking with its confirm open). `layout="row"` keeps each button its own, spaced and wrapping, for a card's few labelled actions (a booking's Reschedule, Copy guest link and Cancel booking on Bookings), so a screen never spaces buttons with markup of its own. `layout="line"` is the same row that never wraps, for a Table's fitted cell, as narrow as its content allows, where a wrapping row stacked a request's Approve and Decline (6 November 2026). A `link` leading a line stands its word on the edge of what's above it, its padding and border outside that edge: a step up's Use a backup code, at the start of the code boxes.
- `SplitButton` when the primary action has variants (Save, Save as new view). Its `menu` is a `Menu`; the chevron is named "More options" unless `menuLabel` says otherwise.
- Labels are verbs in sentence case: "Add to list", never "ADD" or "Submit".
- `CopyButton` says it worked in place (the owner, 22 October 2026: a toast far from the button went unseen): the icon turns to a check and the label to "Copied" for two seconds, at the width it had (Button's `labelReserve` keeps the longer label's room from the start, so nothing beside it moves), and a live region tells screen readers its `announcement` ("Link copied"; "Copied" by default). A refusal says "Couldn't copy. Select the link and copy it yourself." in an error toast and selects the source text; `failureMessage` replaces it where no link is shown to select (a record page's Copy link points to the address bar). "Copied" sits by its check, at the start of the reserved room. Every copy in the app goes through it: Field's `copy` variant, Code, LinkCard, the editors' header, a record page's header. `isLabelCollapsible` passes Button's own through, so in a narrow bar (an editor's header on a phone) only the icon shows, still named by `name` when it has one, else `label` (22 October 2026: the form's name was cut to one letter beside the words; 5 November 2026: a link page's address pill keeps "timefix.com/ada, copy link" when it folds to its icon). `value` may be a read in place of the text (`() => Promise<string>`, 4 November 2026: a booking's guest link, which carries the guest's key and is read only on the press, used to copy from a menu with a toast): the clipboard is handed the read itself, so the write still counts as the press in Safari, and the button is busy until it lands; a failed read is the same refusal. Stories: Default, Copied, CopiedFromRead, IconOnly, LabelCollapsible, AddressPill, Failed, Disabled.
- A brand's mark alone (`provider` with `label`, no `children`) is an icon only button or link with the mark in the icon's place: a host's LinkedIn in SocialLinks (booking socials, 25 October 2026). Why: the row of a person's social links is marks only, and Button already drew a mark beside a label.
- `isIconMark` on an icon only Button draws its Lucide icon as a mark among brand marks: at the marks' size (`size-icon`) and in the text colour, as the black marks are (a person's website among their social links). Why not a plain ghost icon: at `size-icon-sm` in `text-secondary` it read as a quieter, lesser link beside the brands.
- `relations` on an external ButtonLink adds link types beside `noopener noreferrer`: `me` for a person's own profile, `nofollow` for a link a visitor didn't write.
- `labelReserve` on any Button keeps room for a second label it may swap to.
- `isSelectionStrong` on a ToggleButton fills it with the accent when held (on-accent words), instead of the soft accent tint: a public reservation page's party size, which sat beside the accent filled day and time and read as unchosen (review, 5 November 2026). ChoiceChips passes it through.
- `dot` (a `Hue`) on a labelled ToggleButton puts a colour dot before its label, drawn by StatusDot (`isInherited`) as SegmentedControl's `hue` is, loaded with the first chip that wears one so plain buttons never carry it: a zone's colour on its chip (ChoiceChips' `dot`, Menu QR's zones, 5 November 2026). Decorative; the label names it. Why here: ChoiceChips' chips are ToggleButtons, and a chip drawing its own dot would be a second chip style and a third dot.
- `animateLabel` draws a changed label through TextSwap: the old words rise out and fade, and the new ones rise in (spec 0068, AC-6806: Publish → Published). Pair it with `labelReserve` so the width never changes. Reduced motion crossfades.
- `amount` puts a worded amount after the label ("Add to your picks · £9.50", spec 0065): its changed digits pop in through NumberPop, it sits at the end of a full width button, and screen readers hear it in the name after a " · ". Why a prop: `children` is a string, and a price that pops is part of the button, not beside it.
- Both are fenced (audit, 5 November 2026): a Button without `animateLabel` or `amount` draws and moves exactly as before, loads neither TextSwap nor NumberPop, and keeps the booking page's label swap (its 2px blur) untouched.
- `CopyButton` takes `isDisabled` when there is nothing to copy yet (a form never published, a row still on its way), so a row of actions keeps its shape.

Props: `children` (the label, a string) or `icon` with `label` (icon only), or `children` with `label` (a short label with a fuller name); `icon`, `iconRight`; `fieldName` (with `variant="field"`, the name inside its box); `provider` (a brand's mark in the icon's place); `variant`; `size` (spec 0072's heights, full size then the app's: `md` 28 and 24, `lg` 36 and 32, beside an input and pressed 2px past its border all round through an invisible `::after`, so a card footer's controls can't be missed (the owner, 20 October 2026), `sm` a chip's 20 (18 in the app), pressed across a 28px control's height, for an icon only Remove beside a chip inside a field's box, such as a picker's chosen member; why: a 26px button in a 30px box crowded the chip it removes; `xl` 44 (38 in the app) with the heading's 15px label, only for a page's own call to action outside the app: the marketing site's hero and footer (23 October 2026); why: its 56px headline dwarfed a 30px button, and the app's screens never use it; `touch` 44 (`size-control-lg`), only for a public page's control under a thumb: a link page's Save contact, Share and social links (25 October 2026); why: at `lg` the One at a time OK and Start were nearly square and shorter than the field above them, and every hosted form button should be one size; `sheet` 48 (`size-control-lg` and `space-4`) with the heading's 15px label, only for the one action at a guest sheet's foot: the guest menu's Add to your selection (5 November 2026); why: the owner asked for checklabs' guest menu exactly, whose Add is a 48px pill with a 15px label, and `touch` is 44px with the body label; `touchTarget` (`md`, `lg` and `xl` only: drawn at its size, pressed across 44px through an invisible `::after`, for a small control a guest presses with a thumb: the guest menu's + and steppers, drawn at checklabs' 26px, 30px and 36px (5 November 2026); why: the owner asked for checklabs' sizes, and a thumb still needs 44px); an icon only `xl` is a 44 circle (a Stepper's `md` buttons); `isRound` (kept for its callers: every button is a pill since spec 0072, so it changes nothing); `markTone` with `provider` alone (`mono` draws the brand's shape in the label's colour, for a row of marks that must read as one set: a link page's social links); `kbd` (one keycap or several); `onPress`; `isDisabled`; `isPending`; `type`; `form`; `aria-describedby`; `isFullWidth`; `isLabelCollapsible`; `tooltipWhenCut`; `slot`; `ref`.

- `isFullWidth` fills the row, with the label at the start and the keycaps at the end, for the sidebar's Quick actions. `isFullWidth="center"` fills the row with the label in the middle: a Form's submit on a sign in page, and the other ways to go on under it (Continue with Google). A container never stretches a button itself; it asks for this.
- `form` is the `id` of the form a `type="submit"` button sends when it sits outside it: a Modal's footer submitting the Form in its body (see Form's README).
- `label` beside `children` (on Button, ToggleButton and ButtonLink alike) names the button more fully than its visible word: Captures' create button reads "New" on both tabs, so switching tabs moves nothing (the owner, 21 October 2026), and is named "New booking page" or "New form". The name must start with the visible label, so speech input users can say what they see (WCAG 2.5.3, label in name). Why not a longer label: the tabs' buttons would differ in width.
- `aria-describedby` points at text that says more about the button, most often the line under a disabled one that says when it turns on ("You can send another in 42 seconds."), so a screen reader reads the two together.

## States

- **Hover**: `surface-hover` (the primary darkens to `accent-hover`), only on devices that can hover.
- **Pressed**: no shrink (the owner, 20 October 2026); a press reads through the hover and active colours. `scale-press` is 1, so nothing in the library scales on press.
- **Focus**: from the keyboard only, a 1px `accent-line` border (3:1 on every surface) plus the faint `shadow-focus` halo. Primary and danger keep their edge (blue, red), so their 1px `accent-line` ring sits just outside, past a 1px gap. In forced colours, a 2px `Highlight` outline.
- **Disabled**: at `opacity-disabled`, skipped by Tab, never pressed. When a line beside it says why or until when, `aria-describedby` ties that line to it.
- **Pending** (`isPending`): a spinner in place of the icon, presses ignored, still focusable, at `opacity-busy`. Screen readers hear the change. Change the label to the ongoing verb ("Saving"), unless the button must hold its width (Captures' New): then keep the label and give the button a `label`, and React Aria adds the spinner's "In progress" to its name ("New form In progress"). Without a `label`, whether "In progress" joins the name depends on the browser, so a kept label alone wouldn't reliably say anything changed.
- **Selected** (`ToggleButton`): `accent-soft` with an `accent` edge, which holds 3:1 against the surface (the artifact marks a pressed day with the accent too). It keeps that look while hovered. `aria-pressed` tells screen readers.
- **Shortcut** (`kbd`): the keycaps are for sight. The button states the shortcut through `aria-keyshortcuts` (`⌘↵` is `Meta+Enter`), so screen readers hear "Add to list", not "Add to list command return". On a touch only device (no hover, a coarse pointer) the keycaps are hidden: there are no keys to press. In a dialog at phone width (under 480px, where the footer stacks its buttons) they are hidden too, through Modal's public `modal-scrim` container (22 October 2026). Inside a Modal's footer the keys come by default (30 October 2026): a `slot="close"` button shows `ESC` (unless it is the commit, a `primary` or `danger-solid` that also closes), a `type="submit"` button shows `↵` (`⌘↵` when the dialog holds a long text or an editor, and always when it submits a library Form by `form`), and a plain confirm's `primary` shows `↵` and takes focus as the dialog opens, and any other dialog's `primary` that acts on press shows `⌘↵`, which the Modal presses (never in an alert; 5 November 2026), so callers stop typing them; a `kbd` of its own wins, and `kbd={[]}` shows none. Why a default: 70 hand typed `kbd="ESC"` drifted, and only a few primaries showed their key, which Attio shows on every one.

## Press

Held, a button eases to `--scale-press`. The token is 1, so nothing in the app shrinks (20 October 2026). A public booking page sets it to 0.97 on its own root (1 November 2026), so its buttons answer a press there and only there. Reduced motion keeps every button still. The press eases on `--ease-press` when a page sets one, else `ease-out`: the guest menu sets checklabs' overshoot (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`, written by the menu's look stylesheet, since CSS here takes curves only from tokens), so its +, steppers and Add settle with a little spring (5 November 2026).

## Keyboard

- `Tab` and `Shift Tab` reach it (not when disabled).
- `Enter` and `Space` press it. On a `ToggleButton` they toggle it.
- `isLabelCollapsible` hides the label when the button's container is under 480px (a phone), leaving the icon alone in an icon only square; give it an `icon` and a `label`, which stays its name. Why a variant: the view bar's View settings must fit a phone beside the switcher and Table or Board, and only the button can hide its own label (the owner, 22 October 2026). While the label is hidden, hovering the icon shows it in a tooltip, the same one `tooltipWhenCut` opens (22 October 2026).
- `tooltipWhenCut` shows the whole label in a tooltip when the button is held narrower than its label (with `isFullWidth`, in a fixed width cell), measured only as the tooltip is about to open, like TruncatedText. Why: the sort and filter builders hold their attribute pickers to one width so rows line up (spec 0020), and a cut name must still be readable; a tooltip can't sit inside a button, so the button wraps itself.
- `autoFocus` gives it focus when it mounts: the safe choice a dialog opens on (Cancel beside Replace, spec 0020), since a dialog otherwise focuses itself and the first Tab lands on its close button.
- On a `SplitButton`, `Tab` moves from the action to the chevron; `Enter`, `Space` or `↓` opens the menu, `Esc` closes it and returns focus to the chevron. Disabled or pending, both halves wait together.

## Differences from the artifact

- `onPress` replaces `onClick`, and `isDisabled` and `isPending` replace `disabled` and `loading` (React Aria's names, so props pass straight through). Pending keeps the button focusable, where the artifact disabled it.
- `icon` and `iconRight` take registry names (`IconName`), so a typo is a type error.
- `provider` puts a brand's own mark (ProviderMark) where the icon goes, for an action named after that brand: `<Button provider="google">Continue with Google</Button>`, Add to Google Calendar. It is decorative there, since the label names the brand. Why a prop and not an `icon`: the marks are the brands' own artwork, not Lucide glyphs, so they never join the icon registry.
- The label is `children: string`. An icon only button takes `label` instead, and TypeScript refuses one without it; beside `children`, `label` gives a fuller name.
- No `className`, `style` or other HTML attributes pass through: one look, set here.
- `ButtonLink` is new (spec 0025, the guest's booking page): the same looks and sizes on a real link, so a new tab, a copied address and a download work as the browser's own. `href` passes `safeHref`; one that fails draws disabled.
- `ToggleButton` is new (the artifact had no pressed state), for format toolbars and view toggles.
- `SplitButton` takes its `menu` instead of an `onMenu` callback, so the menu opens from the chevron with focus handled by React Aria.
- No `disabledReason` tooltip yet: a disabled button gets no hover or focus, so a tooltip on it would reach no one by keyboard. Until a disabled button can stay focusable, write the reason beside it and tie it with `aria-describedby`, or put a `LockReason` beside it.
- The keycaps no longer read as part of the name, and the primary's keycap is edged instead of filled, so its text holds 4.5:1 (on the `on-accent-kbd` fill it was 3.3:1).
- The SplitButton divider on the primary uses `on-accent-kbd`, as the artifact does.

A label that changes after the first paint resolves in over `--label-swap` (a 2px blur and a fade, a fade alone under reduced motion). The property is registered at 0s, so nothing moves in the app; the public booking page sets it on BookingFrame's root.

## Status dot

`dot` on Button draws a status dot before the label, as ToggleButton's does, in the label's type and colour: where something stands, on the control that changes it (a menu's Live or Hidden, 6 November 2026). Decorative: the label says it too. With a label only. With `animateLabel` the dot's colour fades and the word swaps when it changes (StatusDot's `isAnimated`): Bookings › Pages' Live or Hidden pill, 9 November 2026.

## Timefix prototype (spec 0072)

Takes its look from the prototype's Button: pills, an ink `primary` (`--primary` on `--on-primary`), a white `secondary` with a hairline and `shadow-xs`, a grey `soft`, a bare `ghost` and a red `danger-solid`. Blue is never a button fill; it marks a selection only (a held ToggleButton wears `accent-soft` and `accent-text`, a strong selection the accent fill).

**Use it for**: `primary` for the one thing the view is for (Save changes, Book, Create), at most one per region; `secondary` for every other visible action; `soft` for a quiet action inside a card (Next, Complete); `ghost` in toolbars, rows and icon only buttons; `danger-solid` only for the last step of something that cannot be undone. **Don't** fill a button with blue or put two primaries side by side.

**States**: rest; hover (the fill washes in over `--duration-quick` on `--ease-smooth-out`); pressed (the fill deepens to `surface-selected`, `primary-hover` on ink; nothing scales in the app, `--scale-press` is 1, and a public page sets its own); focus visible (the outline ring, `--border-width-focus` in `--focus-ring`, 2px out); pending (the spinner takes the icon's place, presses are ignored); disabled (`--opacity-disabled`, still focusable for its tooltip). A trigger whose menu is open stays pressed (`surface-selected`).

**Differences from the prototype**
- Sizes keep the library's names (spec 0072's prop map): `md` (the default) is the prototype's `sm` (28 full, 24 in the app), `lg` its `md` (36, 32), `xl` its `lg` (44, 38). `sm` (a chip's Remove), `touch`, `public` and `sheet` are the library's own, drawn in the prototype's language.
- `danger-solid` is the prototype's `danger`. The library's `danger` (red words and edge on the white pill), `ghost-danger`, `dashed` (the prototype's Add row pattern), `link`, `value`, `warning` and `field` (the prototype's filled input, its value at the start) are kept.
- Danger's hover mixes 12% of the text colour into the red (`color-mix`), darker in light and lighter in dark, instead of the prototype's `filter: brightness(0.94)`, which also darkened the focus ring.
- Pending no longer dims the button (the prototype doesn't); the spinner says it.
- Kept beyond the prototype: keycaps and `aria-keyshortcuts`, a Modal footer's keys, `labelReserve` and `animateLabel`, an `amount`, `dot`, `tooltipWhenCut`, `isLabelCollapsible`, `touchTarget`, ButtonLink, ToggleButton, SplitButton, ButtonGroup and CopyButton.
- Owed: the prototype's `collapse="phone"` (below 640px of screen) has no prop yet; `isLabelCollapsible` covers a container under 480px.
