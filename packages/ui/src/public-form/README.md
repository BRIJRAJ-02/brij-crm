# Hosted form

`@crm/ui/public-form`: the form a visitor fills in (spec 0023, `0023-experience.md`, The visitor, and `0023-form-design.md`), on the hosted page at `/f/<address>`, inside a site's embed, and in the builder's preview. All three draw `HostedForm`, so they are one component tree.

- `mountHostedForm(root, form, { mode, countryGuess?, onSubmit?, onRedirect?, logo? })` mounts HostedForm inside UiProviderBase (UiProvider without the icon registry, the brand artwork or the toast region, none of which the form draws) into a new element at the end of `root`, synchronously, so the page can measure it for an embed's height at once. It answers `{ update(form), destroy() }`. `mode` is `page` (an h1 at `text-title-lg`, full height, a centred card from the page breakpoint), `embed` (an h2 at `text-title`, no card, no page height) or `preview` (inert, never submits, a visually hidden note says it is a preview).
- `mountFormLoading(root, mode, layout?)` draws the form's shape before the form itself has arrived: the same ground, the same card, and the places the headline, the sub heading, three questions and the button will take. The page mounts it the moment its script runs, so a visitor's first paint is the page rather than a blank window (24 October 2026, the owner: "no flash no nothing"), and replaces it with the form, the message or the closed note in the same frame, so the page never shows both and never shows neither. It carries no `data-theme`: the form's Colour is not known yet, and guessing one would mean taking it back. With a step layout it draws that layout's shape (Steps' progress line over two questions and Continue, or One at a time's line and one question with no card), which `mountHostedForm` also shows while that layout's chunk loads (spec 0063, AC-6306).
- `mountFormMessage(root, { mode, kind })` draws a message in the form's place: not found, or a page that couldn't load.
- `HostedFormPreview` (a module) renders HostedForm directly for the builder.

## Why it uses the library

Until 19 October 2026 this was plain DOM, drawn to look like the library, to stay under a 40 kB budget. The owner asked for one input system everywhere, so it now draws with the library's own Field, Select, CountryPicker, PhoneField, RadioGroup, Checkbox, Switch and Button. The page loads React and React Aria, under its own budget in `apps/web/.size-limit.js` (223 kB JavaScript, 17 kB CSS, gzipped), and the step layouts as a lazy chunk with its own 10 kB line. The phone library loads later, on demand.

## The answer types of spec 0063

Date, Time and Date and time are the platform's own inputs in a Field (`temporal`): its picker, its keyboard and its phone wheel, no date library on the page. A date and time sends its moment with the visitor's time zone. A choice is RadioGroup's `letters` variant in every layout: full width options with their letter keys, a letter picking one (T42). A yes or no is the same two options, Yes and No, rather than a switch, which read as a setting (critique c, 9); nothing picked still sends No. Rating is the Rating atom as a form question (`field`), five 24px stars that are a radio group; every question type draws its error with the one FieldError line (the alert mark, then the words); Scale is ChoiceChips with one choice and its end words under the row (`ends`); Multi select is ChoiceChips with Other opening a box under it. Address is `AddressQuestion`. A hidden question draws nothing and takes the link's value, else its default. Each is checked by `checkAnswers`, as the server checks it. None of them is heavy, so none is a lazy chunk.

## Jumps, endings and recall (spec 0063 slice 5)

A step's (and, with One at a time, a question's) jumps ride in the public form, so the page walks the same path the
server will: `pathOf` takes the first jump whose conditions hold, forward only, and a jump to an ending ends the
form there, which shows as the steps shrinking and the step the visitor is on becoming the last. Nothing about the
path or the score is the page's to decide: the server works both out again from the answers it stored
(`landed` in `@crm/core`), drops the answers to questions off the path and files its own score.

A label, a help line and an ending's words may recall an earlier answer or the score with `{{key}}` and
`{{score}}`. `recall` gives plain text back and the page draws it as text, so a name holding `<b>` shows its angle
brackets. Nothing here ever builds HTML. An ending's own heading, line and web-address button come with the
submit's next step; an ending that books a meeting arrives as the booking next step, since only the server signs a
hand off.

## Rules it follows

- Every word comes from `formStrings` in `@crm/contracts/forms`, and every check from `checkAnswers`, the one the submit endpoint runs again, so a visitor sees the server's own message.
- Labels sit above inputs, at the body size inside the card (the card points both label tokens at `text-body`, 22 October 2026: the app's dense label size was too quiet for a visitor, and a choice group's medium question sat beside regular ones). The line under a field follows at the same size: an error by its colour and icon, a hint by its place under the field. Inside the card a link Button and a Field's prefix read regular too. The foot under the card keeps the small label. An optional question's label is followed by a quiet "(optional)" in `text-tertiary` (each control's `labelAside`), part of its name; required ones carry nothing, and neither does a tick box, a consent or a yes or no. A consent (spec 0023, AC-1053) is drawn as the library's Checkbox with its exact wording as the label, so a required one says "Tick this to go on." as a required tick box does. A dropdown or country with no answer reads "Choose one".
- **The field checker**: a question is checked when focus leaves it with something in it (or when a required one is emptied again), and every question on submit. A shown error clears the moment the answer passes, but a new one never appears while typing. Errors sit under their field; a submit that finds problems focuses the first and lists them above the button ("2 answers need a look"), each a link that focuses its question, announced politely. Failures that aren't about a question show in one `role="alert"` line above the button. The button stays enabled.
- **Email typos**: an address whose domain is a common provider misspelt ("gmial.com") gets "Did you mean ada@gmail.com?" with Use it (`suggestEmail`), a hint and never an error.
- While sending, the button keeps its width, shows the spinner and reads "Sending…"; a second press does nothing.
- A phone answer is `{ number, country }` with the number in E.164 (`toE164`); a country answer is the country's name in the form's language. PhoneField is used unchanged, so its own per country example and check arrive here when they land.
- The honeypot, the fill time and the idempotency round work as before (a new round gets a new idempotency key).
- A required question left empty nudges once beside its line, a short shake through the Web Animations API (none under reduced motion), so the stylesheet carries none of it. Finish later is one quiet line, a tick and Saved beside it, that opens Copy link and Email it to me only when pressed and never shows the raw link; a failed save or email says what happened. A form that fails to load offers Try again.
- The thank you replaces the form in place, with focus on its heading, in the booking confirmation's shape: a green tinted check, "Thanks, we got it.", a muted line and what was sent, with no Send another (T23, Tally's way: a visitor who sent it is done); an ending's own button when it has one. What was sent (spec 0058, AC-19) reads back each shown question that has an answer, its label beside it (`SentAnswers.tsx`, a plain list with Card's sunken look and DescriptionList's rhythm: those two cost the hosted page 1.3 kB of JS and 1.3 kB of CSS its budget doesn't have, so this is a deliberate exception to the no new CSS for a library element rule, to fold into DescriptionList once there is room), from the answers the page already holds: no request, and nothing the visitor didn't just type. A tick box or a yes or no is left out (its label is the whole answer), a file shows its name, a phone its number. A label takes at most two fifths of the row, and an answer shows its first three lines.
- The look's third axis is Colours (spec 0063, AC-6327): `brand` wears the workspace's kit, `plain` the product's own. Nothing here reads it. The form's own read carries `brand` (its stylesheet version and its logo version) only when the look is Brand, so a plain form arrives with no kit at all and `FormGround` draws no logo; the accent comes from the page's served `brand.css`, never from anything the page is told.
- The look maps onto tokens, with no CSS of its own for a control: `data-theme` (Colour) redefines the tokens for light or dark, and `data-corners='square'` (Corners) points the radius tokens at the smallest step, so every control inside squares its corners. Every hosted form reads at its public size (the design critique, 5 November 2026, after Typeform and Tally): 16px body and input text, 40px fields and buttons (44px on a touch screen, so a finger finds them and phones don't zoom into a field), and questions named in the text colour (FieldLabel's `--label-colour`), never the app's dense 13px, 30px and grey; until the design system has a public type size token, it is drawn from the spacing scale.
- "Powered by" and the Timefix wordmark sit centred under the card, in the booking pages' foot's small secondary label and size (spec 0058, AC-26) (or under the form in an embed), its words going to full ink when pointed at, linking to www.timefix.com with the footer campaign tags (`poweredByHref('form')` from `lib/powered-by.ts`, the same helper the booking guest pages use; in a new tab inside an embed). It defaults to the web app's own files in `public/brand`.
- It never touches the network: the page's client sends the answers through `onSubmit`.

## States

Loading, before the form has been read (`HostedForm.stories.tsx`). Book after sending and its Stay here (`HostedForm.stories.tsx`; the countdown itself in `FormBooking.browser.test.tsx`). Classic, every answer type, embed, dark and square, errors under each question (no summary since 5 November 2026: it only repeated them, and focus already goes to the first), checked on blur, the email hint, sending, failed, thanks, paused (`HostedForm.stories.tsx`). Steps, a step's errors, the second step, the last step and its thanks, steps dark and square, one at a time, its next question, its error, one at a time dark, a step its Show when skips, a choice that goes on by itself and a long answer's Shift and Enter (`HostedFormSteps.stories.tsx`). Each layout's loading shape (`FormLoading.browser.test.tsx`).

## Keyboard

Tab follows the questions. A letter key picks a lettered choice (and, One at a time, goes on by itself as a pick by pointer does). Enter in a single line field submits (Classic), continues (Steps, One at a time) or sends on the last step. One at a time, Enter on a choice or in a long answer continues too, and Shift and Enter breaks a long answer's line. The summary's links focus their questions. A step change moves focus to its first question and announces its place politely.

## Brand kit

A live form wears its workspace's kit (spec 0060, AC-4). `formBrand(form)` turns the read's `brand.logo` version into the logo's path on our own origin (`/api/public/forms/<key>/logo?v=`), drawn over the form at a control's height; the page's brand stylesheet brings the accent, which `FormGround` points the library's own names at inside the card, exactly as BookingFrame does, with every name falling back to its token. An app preview passes `brand` (`PageBrand`: a logo and the accent's `variables`) instead.

A form that wears its kit wears its workspace's template too (spec 0068): `formBrand` carries `brand.template` and `FormGround` sets `data-template` (and Night's or Studio's `data-theme`, which wins over the form's own Colour) with the ground and the headline's face. A plain form names no kit and so no template.

- **A question's description** (5 November 2026): `description` on a question is a quiet line in the secondary colour: under its control in Classic and Steps (`QuestionSlot`), and under the large heading with One at a time (`isDescriptionShown={false}` on the slot there), with recalls read in it.

## A file that didn't upload, and a closed form's words (forms fixes b8, b2, 5 November 2026)

- A file question names what it takes under its drop area ("PDF, images or Office files, up to 4 MB"). An upload the page's client answers `unavailable` (the form has no file store, 503) says the form can't take files now and that the answers can still go, never "try again". A failed file marks its question (`HostedFileHandlers.setFailed`, optional): the first press of Send then says the file didn't upload and that pressing again sends the rest without it, so a file is never left behind in silence. Test: `FileFailure.browser.test.tsx`.
- A closed form (paused, or past its own closing rule) shows the owner's `closedMessage` when the form has one, else the language's line (`HostedFormState.closedLine`).
