# Hosted form

`@crm/ui/public-form`: the form a visitor fills in (spec 0023, `0023-experience.md`, The visitor, and `0023-form-design.md`), on the hosted page at `/f/<address>`, inside a site's embed, and in the builder's preview. All three draw `HostedForm`, so they are one component tree.

- `mountHostedForm(root, form, { mode, countryGuess?, onSubmit?, onRedirect?, logo? })` mounts HostedForm inside UiProviderBase (UiProvider without the icon registry, the brand artwork or the toast region, none of which the form draws) into a new element at the end of `root`, synchronously, so the page can measure it for an embed's height at once. It answers `{ update(form), destroy() }`. `mode` is `page` (an h1 at `text-note-title`, full height, a centred card from the page breakpoint), `embed` (an h2 at `text-heading`, no card, no page height) or `preview` (inert, never submits, a visually hidden note says it is a preview).
- `mountFormLoading(root, mode, layout?)` draws the form's shape before the form itself has arrived: the same ground, the same card, and the places the headline, the sub heading, three questions and the button will take. The page mounts it the moment its script runs, so a visitor's first paint is the page rather than a blank window (24 October 2026, the owner: "no flash no nothing"), and replaces it with the form, the message or the closed note in the same frame, so the page never shows both and never shows neither. It carries no `data-theme`: the form's Colour is not known yet, and guessing one would mean taking it back. With a step layout it draws that layout's shape (Steps' progress line over two questions and Continue, or One at a time's line and one question with no card), which `mountHostedForm` also shows while that layout's chunk loads (spec 0063, AC-6306).
- `mountFormMessage(root, { mode, kind })` draws a message in the form's place: not found, or a page that couldn't load.
- `HostedFormPreview` (a module) renders HostedForm directly for the builder.

## Why it uses the library

Until 19 October 2026 this was plain DOM, drawn to look like the library, to stay under a 40 kB budget. The owner asked for one input system everywhere, so it now draws with the library's own Field, Select, CountryPicker, PhoneField, RadioGroup, Checkbox, Switch and Button. The page loads React and React Aria, under its own budget in `apps/web/.size-limit.js` (223 kB JavaScript, 17 kB CSS, gzipped), and the step layouts as a lazy chunk with its own 10 kB line. The phone library loads later, on demand.

## The answer types of spec 0063

Date, Time and Date and time are the platform's own inputs in a Field (`temporal`): its picker, its keyboard and its phone wheel, no date library on the page. A date and time sends its moment with the visitor's time zone. Rating is the Rating atom as a form question (`field`), five stars that are a radio group; Scale is ChoiceChips with one choice and its end words under the row (`ends`); Multi select is ChoiceChips with Other opening a box under it. Address is `AddressQuestion`. A hidden question draws nothing and takes the link's value, else its default. Each is checked by `checkAnswers`, as the server checks it. None of them is heavy, so none is a lazy chunk.

## Rules it follows

- Every word comes from `formStrings` in `@crm/contracts/forms`, and every check from `checkAnswers`, the one the submit endpoint runs again, so a visitor sees the server's own message.
- Labels sit above inputs, at the body size inside the card (the card points both label tokens at `text-body`, 22 October 2026: the app's dense label size was too quiet for a visitor, and a choice group's medium question sat beside regular ones). The line under a field follows at the same size: an error by its colour and icon, a hint by its place under the field. Inside the card a link Button and a Field's prefix read regular too. The foot under the card keeps the small label. An optional question's label is followed by a quiet "(optional)" in `text-tertiary` (each control's `labelAside`), part of its name; required ones carry nothing, and neither does a tick box or a yes or no. A dropdown or country with no answer reads "Choose one".
- **The field checker**: a question is checked when focus leaves it with something in it (or when a required one is emptied again), and every question on submit. A shown error clears the moment the answer passes, but a new one never appears while typing. Errors sit under their field; a submit that finds problems focuses the first and lists them above the button ("2 answers need a look"), each a link that focuses its question, announced politely. Failures that aren't about a question show in one `role="alert"` line above the button. The button stays enabled.
- **Email typos**: an address whose domain is a common provider misspelt ("gmial.com") gets "Did you mean ada@gmail.com?" with Use it (`suggestEmail`), a hint and never an error.
- While sending, the button keeps its width, shows the spinner and reads "Sending…"; a second press does nothing.
- A phone answer is `{ number, country }` with the number in E.164 (`toE164`); a country answer is the country's name in the form's language. PhoneField is used unchanged, so its own per country example and check arrive here when they land.
- The honeypot, the fill time and the idempotency round work as before: "Send another" starts a new round, which the page gives a new idempotency key.
- The thank you replaces the form in place, with focus on its heading, in the booking confirmation's shape: a green tinted check, the heading, a muted line, then Send another.
- The look maps onto tokens, with no CSS of its own for a control: `data-theme` (Colour) redefines the tokens for light or dark, and `data-corners='square'` (Corners) points the radius tokens at the smallest step, so every control inside squares its corners. On touch screens the form's body and input text is 16px and its inputs taller, so phones don't zoom into a field; until the design system has a touch type size token, that size is drawn from the spacing scale (pending owner confirmation).
- The Timefix wordmark sits centred under the card (or under the form in an embed), quiet until pointed at, linking to www.timefix.com with the footer campaign tags (`poweredByHref('form')` from `lib/powered-by.ts`, the same helper the booking guest pages use; in a new tab inside an embed). It defaults to the web app's own files in `public/brand`.
- It never touches the network: the page's client sends the answers through `onSubmit`.

## States

Loading, before the form has been read (`HostedForm.stories.tsx`). Book after sending and its Stay here (`HostedForm.stories.tsx`; the countdown itself in `FormBooking.browser.test.tsx`). Classic, every answer type, embed, dark and square, errors with the summary, checked on blur, the email hint, sending, failed, thanks, paused (`HostedForm.stories.tsx`). Steps, a step's errors, the second step, the last step and its thanks, steps dark and square, one at a time, its next question, its error, one at a time dark, a step its Show when skips, a choice that goes on by itself and a long answer's Shift and Enter (`HostedFormSteps.stories.tsx`). Each layout's loading shape (`FormLoading.browser.test.tsx`).

## Keyboard

Tab follows the questions. Enter in a single line field submits (Classic), continues (Steps, One at a time) or sends on the last step. One at a time, Enter on a choice or in a long answer continues too, and Shift and Enter breaks a long answer's line. The summary's links focus their questions. A step change moves focus to its first question and announces its place politely.
