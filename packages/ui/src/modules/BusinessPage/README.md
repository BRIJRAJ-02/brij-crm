# BusinessPage

A business's public page at `/<company-handle>` and each place at `/<company-handle>/<place-slug>` (spec 0066, B4 lite; the owner's first go live, R1 in spec 0068), after Fresha's venue page: the gallery, the name, what it is and whether it is open, the address with Directions, section tabs that follow the scroll, then the Menu, About (with amenities), Opening hours and Location; a panel that stays beside the sections on a laptop, a bar at the foot of a phone's screen carrying Reserve a table, the places picker when the business has several, and the foot (the public page passes `BookingFoot`, tagged `business_page`).

## Why it exists

New (4 November 2026). The link page (`LinkPage`) is a person's list of things to press, in one thumb wide column; a business page is a venue's facts read at a glance and a single action (Reserve, later Book), laid out as a page with a side panel on a wide screen. Bending LinkPage into two columns, a mosaic, a scroll spy and a sticky action bar would have changed every person's page; so this is its own module, built from the library's atoms (Button, ButtonLink, Icon, Skeleton), the Modal (the photo viewer) and BookingFrame's foot. Its block parts are exported on their own so the editor's preview and later page kinds (events, spec 0066 B8) draw the same ones:

- **BusinessGallery**: up to 20 photos. From a laptop up a large first photo and up to four tiles in the page's large corners (`radius-xl`), with "See all N photos" over the last; below that a full width swipe row that snaps a photo at a time with its place ("2 / 8") in the corner. Any photo opens the viewer: the library's Modal (`window`, `isWide`) holding one photo large, Previous and Next, the arrow keys and a swipe, and focus back on the photo it showed. A photo that fails to load leaves the gallery; with none left it draws nothing. The first photo loads eagerly at high priority (the page's largest paint), every other lazily.
- **BusinessMenu**: a published menu's sections (0065 H1), each item's name and price on one baseline with a dotted run between them as on a printed menu, its description quiet under it. Read only: ordering is the Menu app's page. Nothing for a menu with no items.
- **BusinessHours**: the week from Monday as a description list, each day's ranges in tabular figures, today in the strong weight with a "Today" pill, a closed day said so. Today reads its own date, so an exception today (closed for a holiday) shows. Times are the place's wall clock; a visitor in another zone is told whose ("Times in Kolkata time").
- **BusinessLocation**: the address beside its pin, the phone (`tel:`) and site as quiet links, then Directions to Google Maps and Apple Maps side by side at `touch` height, each in a new tab. No embedded map (AC-6645): it would add a tracking origin and a hole in the page's policy.
- **BusinessReserve**: the page's one primary. `open` is a link to the reservation flow; `soon` (until the reservation lane lands) is the same button disabled with a "Coming soon" pill and a line saying to call meanwhile, so a guest is never left at a dead end. The phone's bar leaves the line out (`showsNote={false}`).

## Anatomy and behaviour

- **Open status** is computed in the browser from the place's hours and the provider's clock, with contracts' `openAt` and `nextOpening` (the server's own rules): "Open now · Closes at 23:00", or "Closed · Opens at 18:00", "Opens tomorrow at 12:00", "Opens Monday at 12:00". So an edge cached page is never wrong about whether it is open.
- **Section tabs** are a `nav` of links to the sections (not React Aria Tabs: they move through one page, they don't switch panels), sticky under the top. The current one (`aria-current="location"`) is the last section whose top passed under the tabs, read by an IntersectionObserver, so scrolling costs nothing. The indicator slides between them on `duration-move` and `ease-in-out`, positioned through the CSS object model as React Aria's own tab indicator is, so the page's `style-src` is unchanged; still under reduced motion. On a phone the tabs scroll sideways and the current one is kept in view. A page with one section has no tabs.
- **Motion**: the header's lines rise in once, a quarter press apart (texts reveal); photos fade in from the sunken surface; the phone's bar rises from the foot on `ease-drawer`; the viewer is the Modal's own panel reveal. All off under reduced motion.
- **Wide and narrow**: one breakpoint, the library's (`1024px`): under it, the gallery is edge to edge and the bar carries Reserve; from it up, the page sits in its margins, the mosaic shows, and the panel (name, open status, Reserve, address) sticks beside the sections under the tabs.
- **States**: `isLoading` draws the gallery's and header's shapes (Skeleton) in the page's own layout. With no photos the header leads; with no menu, About or amenities those sections and their tabs go; with no Table seating there is no Reserve, no panel action and no bar. Errors and "no such page" are the public page's (`BookingMessage`), not this module's.
- **Accessibility**: one `h1` (the name), an `h2` per section with its tab pointing at it, the panel an `aside` named by the business, every photo a button naming its place ("Open photo 2 of 8") and an image with the business's own description or "<name>, photo 2 of 8". Every link out says it opens a new tab.
