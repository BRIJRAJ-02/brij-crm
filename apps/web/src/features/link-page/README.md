# The link page editor (spec 0051, built as blocks)

## Settings › Account › Profile › Link page (`/w/$slug/settings/account/profile?view=link-page`, LinkPageLoader, LinkPageScreen)

- **Purpose**: what the person's page at `/<handle>` holds, in order, beside a phone that shows it as a visitor will.
- **Main task**: add a booking page, a link or a heading, edit it, move it, archive or delete it; every change shows at once, on the list and in the phone, and is saved as it is made.
- **Leaves out**: the bio and the picture (Settings › Profile). A handle is claimed right here when there is none.

**Live or Private** (spec 0058, AC-23, 31 October 2026, the industry standard way): one Switch labelled Live sits first in the bar (`LiveSwitch` and `useLinkPageLive` in `private-page.tsx`, the same pair Home's card uses). Its word never changes with the state, so a screen reader hears "Live, switch, off" rather than a double negative; while it is off, the overview's line and the Share modal say "Private. Only you can open it." A handle the person claims (first run's Your link page, Settings › Profile, the claim here) is live at once, as on Linktree. A page made for them with a membership (a workspace they were invited to, the daily cleanup) starts private and goes live the first time they open this screen, said once in a toast with Undo. After that only the switch changes it: opening, claiming again or editing never overrule a Private choice (`auth.public_profiles.claimed_at`, migration 0154, `crm_show_public_profile` acting only while it is null, `crm_set_public_profile_live` for the switch, both through `profile.show`). Sharing no longer publishes anything: the Share modal says Private in its scan line's place, with Turn on Live beside it, and while the page is private nothing offers its link: the bar shows only the switch, with Private beside it, and the overview's Your page card swaps Share online and Share in person for one line ("Private. Turn on Live to share it.") and Turn on Live, the same switch in words; Edit page and the preview stay. On a phone's narrow bar (under 480px) the address pill, Share and Done drop their words for their icons, keeping their names, so the bar fits a 320px screen. The old "goes public the first time you edit or share" panel is gone.

A view of Profile's (its trail reads Settings › Account › Profile › Link page; Profile stays the current place in the settings sidebar), opened from Profile's Your link page row and the booking editor's Link page card. Why a view and not a route of its own: a route's loader and chunk list sit in the app's first load, which had 300 B less room than a route needs (250 kB budget); the view's store, screen and copy all load with Profile's own chunk and `data.linkPage.load()`. It is a settings page that fills the view (`fillsView`) with PreviewLayout under its bar: the blocks in a Card beside the phone, whose page is the library's LinkPage drawn from the editor's own list (`isPreview`: never a frame, never the public origin); under the layout's width, Preview opens it in a sheet. The bar holds View page (the public page in a new tab).

- **Blocks**: a LinkList with `leadingLoading` (each row's tile is the page's own, `LinkPageTile`), grips (drag, and the keyboard on the grip: Enter, the arrows, Enter), a Copy link button on the row (a link's address, a booking page's `/<handle>/<slug>`; a CopyButton that says Copied in place, no toast, C11, 4 November 2026), and a menu per row: Edit (links and headings), Move up and Move down (only the moves there is room for, announced in a status region), Archive (one press: it can be restored), and Delete, which asks first (`tone="danger"`, "Delete block") and waits there for the server. Pressing a row opens its editor. A paused page shows Paused; a page made Link only, deleted, or in a workspace the person left shows Unavailable, kept until they delete it.
- **Email signup** (spec 0052, AC-5217) is an entry in the Add modal, **not a kind**. It makes a hosted form that asks one thing — an address — publishes it, and then adds an ordinary `form` block pointing at it. So the addresses land in that form's responses, where every other capture on Timefix already lands, and the person's existing routing, notifications and export work on them unchanged. The modal says where they will go before it makes anything, and the workspace picker shows only when the person is in more than one. The form keeps its submissions only, which is what lets it be made in any workspace without asking which object to file against. Because it makes a form block, what it needs room for is the form limit (`blockKindOf`), not a limit of its own. The row's words are checked before anything is made, so a refusal never leaves a stray form behind.
- **A testimonial** (spec 0052, AC-5219): three plain fields — the words, who said them, and what they do. Six live on one page. Its row in the list reads as the author with their first line after, it has no clicks chip because it has nowhere to go, and the modal draws it as the page will. No photo and no logo: the page loads no picture from another origin.
- **A section that folds** (spec 0054, AC-5415): a heading's editor has one more choice, "The blocks under it" — Always shown or Folds shut. It is a word in the heading's own `data`, not a new kind, not a new column and not a level above the list: the blocks under a heading are still the blocks after it, the order stays one flat sequence, and the limits, the reorder statement and the hide rule are untouched. `open` is never stored, so a heading written before folders and an open one written after it are the same row. The editor's preview puts one block under the heading, since a folder with nothing under it draws as a plain heading and the choice would otherwise change nothing on screen.
- **Sections**: a heading block draws as the heading over the rows after it, a small uppercase label with the number of blocks under it (LinkList `variant="section"`). It is still one row of the same list, so it is dragged, moved by the keyboard and counted like any other, which is what a heading is in the data. The screen works out each count, since it is the only thing that knows where the next section starts. With pages from more than one workspace, the card's footer says that pages from another workspace keep their own section and that no visitor sees which workspace a page came from (`footerNote`).
- **Add**: one modal, whatever is being added (the owner's Add item board, 25 October 2026). Add item and New section sit under the list; the modal opens on a grid of every kind as an icon over a short name (`KindPicker`, TileGrid's `choice` variant, spec 0058 AC-9), the line about each kind in its tooltip only, and the chosen kind's fields follow in the same dialog. A full kind's card is disabled with the limit's sentence (60 blocks, 40 links, 12 headings), a button never opens the picker on a kind that is full, and with every kind full Add item is disabled. The id is minted once as the modal opens and kept as the kind changes, so switching twice and saving is still one add. The footer says where the block lands ("It lands at the end. Drag it anywhere after."), or, while a kind is full, the limit's sentence, since a full kind's tile is off and its tooltip out of a keyboard's reach. Editing an existing block opens its own kind's editor, with no picker, exactly as before.
- **Writing** (spec 0052, slice 1): a `text` block is a few lines in the person's own words, a body of up to 1,000 characters with at most 20 line breaks and an optional 60 character title. It draws as prose on the page, not as a row, records no press and shows no click chip. Up to 5 live on a page. Plain words only: there is no markup, so there is nothing to sanitise, and a link is a link block.
- **Questions** (spec 0052, slice 1): a `faq` block is up to 10 questions, each a question of up to 120 characters on one line and an answer of up to 600 with at most 10 line breaks, under a title that defaults to "Questions". Two questions with the same words, ignoring case, are refused before anything is sent. The page draws them as the library's disclosure, closed to begin with; nothing is counted, since a fold is not a destination. Up to 2 sets live on a page. Its editor is a QuestionList: a plus adds a question (opened on its fields), the grip or the arrows on it move one, and Remove in the open card takes it away. Remove sits in the card rather than a row menu, since the card is already open on what it would remove.
- **A Timefix form** (spec 0052, slice 1): a `form` block names one of the person's own hosted forms by its workspace and a real `form_id` column, so a deleted form can't leave a block pointing at nothing and one form is placed once. It draws as a row that opens `/f/:key` in the same tab, on our own origin: no frame, no new policy, and a press counted like any other. `data` holds the owner's own `label` and nothing else; the form's internal name fills the field in the editor but never reaches the public page. Up to 3 live on a page. A form that is not published, is deleted, or whose workspace the person left is skipped by the public read and its block stays, so publishing it again puts it back where it was.
- **Appearance** (spec 0054): a card above the blocks with five named starting points — Plain, Paper, Bold, Ink and Quiet, each a thumbnail of the page in that look with its name under it (`LinkPageThumbnail` in TileGrid's `choice` variant, ringed and ticked when it is the look on the page; spec 0058, AC-10) — and six controls under them — Background, Colour, Light or dark, Corners, Rows and Typeface — and Reset. Every pick is a name from a closed set, never a colour or a size: the look reaches the page as six data attributes and one stylesheet block each, so no CSS is written at run time and the policy is untouched. Each pick shows in the preview at once and saves behind it; a refusal puts the control back and says why on the card. Light or dark is the owner's call, pinned with `data-theme` on the page's own root, so a visitor whose phone is dark still sees the page the owner chose. Picking a starting point writes all six keys at once; **the name is never stored**, and the chip that shows as picked is worked out from the six keys, so a look can never drift out of step with the word that named it, and changing one key simply leaves every chip unpicked.
- **Calm editor** (owner, 5 November 2026: "too much issues and complicated", "lack of hierarchy, too many buttons"; Linktree's and Bento's shape): the bar is the one place to share (Live, the address pill that copies the link in one press, and Share, whose modal holds the QR and Download, Open page, Links for each place, Style the code and Add to Wallet). The overview is Your page (Edit page, the one primary, and the code with Download QR code) and Insights (a comparison only when there was something before; Download CSV an icon). The editor: Blocks with no subtitle, each row's address without its scheme and its clicks as one quiet number at its end (LinkList's `figure`), and one Add item with a menu beside it for New section and Bring a page across; Social links as one line and its button; Design as the four templates in small pictures (the phone is the large one), Colour, the cover as one FileDrop row (`variant="row"`) and Photo shape as two segments; Details (who it's from and sharing and search in one card): Name and Title first, changed right here as on Linktree (9 November 2026; the same name and job title Profile holds, a title typed here turning on and one cleared turning off), the Show under my name switch only while there is a title, no hints that restate labels, and the share image as one FileDrop row.
- **Design** (spec 0068 since 4 November 2026: four templates, colour, cover, face; the gallery below is history): the Appearance card widened into the Design panel. The gallery holds 14 looks (the five above and Dawn, Ocean, Mint, Studio, Editorial, Night, Peach, Graphite and Gallery), each a live `LinkPageThumbnail` drawn by the real stylesheet in the owner's own colour. Picking Gallery before there is a background photo opens Customise, where the photo is added. Customise (closed until opened) holds the page's colour (`AccentPicker`: Workspace first, which follows the workspace's brand, the nine hues, Any colour and the hex, and the guard's one line when it moved the colour, with a Dark colour under it once there is a colour of the page's own), then Background (the four grounds, eight gradients, four patterns and Photo), Font (the eight packs), Buttons, Corners, Header, Face and Mode (Light, Dark, Follow visitor), each a row of thumbnails of the page with that one choice made. A background or cover photo is a FileDrop under its row, placed in the library's cropper (spec 0068, AC-6809: a cover in its 3:1 frame, a background free; "Adjust crop" crops the one there again) and drawn again in the browser by `preparePhoto` from `@crm/ui/photo` (every photo resized, which leaves location and camera behind) and sent through `data.brand`; removing one asks first in a Modal. The colour and photos come from the brand store (`data.brand.load()`, loaded with the link page store), which hands back the same variables the page's `look.css` sets (`brandVariables()`), so the phone, every tile and each block's draft (`draft-look.tsx`, the "How it looks" of every editor) wear exactly what visitors see. A new look remounts the phone, which fades in on its new ground.
- **The figures**: each row's clicks over 30 days are read apart from the blocks and land as the row's quiet number at its end (none for zero); if they fail, the Blocks card's foot says Clicks didn't load.
- **Editors** (`editors.tsx`): one Modal per kind (an add borrows it through `chrome`: the title, the picker above the fields, the footer's line and the primary's word), its form checked by `data.linkPage.check` (the server's rules, before anything is sent: a title, the address rule, a note's length, a heading's text once), with "How it looks" under the fields: the one block drawn as typed by LinkPage's `layout="blocks"` (the unsaved draft, AC-5119), the network's mark included (`data.linkPage.markOf`). A link's address is kept as typed (https added to a bare one): never wrapped. Booking page lists the person's Public pages from every workspace not on the page yet, the workspace named when there are several, read fresh each time it opens; with none left it says so and offers Make a booking page.
- **Bring a page across** (spec 0055, AC-5524 to AC-5528): in the More ways to add menu beside Add item, with New section (owner, 5 November 2026: one Add, as Linktree and Bento have it); on the empty page it stays a button beside Add item. One modal with one field: paste a Linktree, Bio.link, Lnk.Bio, Linktw.in, Liinks, Solo.to or Beacons address, and we read it once and bring its links over. While it reads, the field and Cancel are held and the dialog is busy. A refusal about the address shows under the field and goes the moment it is edited; one that isn't (the page wouldn't answer, the hourly limit) shows as a danger Callout above it, where it can't read as a mistake in what was typed. The import's id is minted per address: a send that failed on the way back (the rows landed, the answer didn't) is tried again under the same id and adds nothing twice, while a corrected address takes a new one, so the server never answers about a page it didn't read. The seven services are one hint line under the field. The answer replaces the field with an announced Callout titled with the count, one line saying the rows wait in Archived, then the list of what came across (each title and where it goes, read from the store's archived blocks by the answer's ids, so no request of its own; spec 0058, AC-16), and the rows that stayed behind as a DescriptionList with a reason each ("it points back at that page", "it's the same link twice"). Every reason the contract has is worded, and typecheck fails if one is added without words, so no row can vanish from that list. Every row lands **archived**, so a person who imports and walks away has published nothing, and the choosing happens in the Archived card with the switch that was already there. Nothing is fetched for an address that isn't one of the seven, and the one bounded fetch is the api's (`src/public/outside.ts`).
- **Archived**: a Card under the blocks while there are any, each with Restore (back to the end) and Delete.
- **Saving**: through `data.linkPage` only (optimistic, in order, put back with a toast and Try again on a refusal).

Every membership's handle and empty page are made with the membership (26 Oct 2026: making a workspace, an invitation, the invite link or an approved request to join; `crm_ensure_public_profile`, migration 0115), so the no handle state is rare: only a page that couldn't be made then (the daily cleanup's `link_pages` phase tries again). Home's Your link page card carries the same Live switch beside Edit, and its no handle state sends the person here, never to Profile.

States: no handle (`ClaimLink`: the link page drawing, one line, and the address field after the public host with Claim beside it on the empty state's centre line; the contract's rule checks it before anything is sent, a server refusal lands on the field, and a claim reads the profile again so the screen opens on the live page; spec 0058, AC-8), loading (`LinkPagePending`: the bar, the overview card busy and the phone's page skeleton, the frame the screen opens on), failed (one row with Try again), empty (the overview's start card and the editor's list each draw the link page drawing, one line and one primary: Add to your page, or Add item beside Bring a page across, with the card's own footer stepping aside meanwhile; the phone's empty page), and the limits. Delete and remove confirm in a Modal with one line and two buttons (`DeleteBlockDialog`, AC-18).

## The registry (`registry.tsx`)

The one place a kind of block plugs into the editor: its Add entry (label, line, icon), its editor Modal, how its row reads, what Copy link copies, and how it draws in the phone. A later kind is one entry here, a schema in `@crm/contracts` (`ProfileBlock`), a renderer in the library's LinkPage, and a line in the public page's `blocksOf`. Planned, not built: photo and video, video and music embeds (the visitor picks their service), testimonials, folders and tabs, an email capture, the contact card (phase 2).

## Spec 0061 in the editor (R4)

- **Six new kinds** in the Add grid (`registry.tsx`, editors in `editors-reach.tsx`): Next times (a booking page's next open times; the phone shows its title and More times, never a time it didn't read), Video or music (a share link from the five services; the draft names the service from the host as it is typed, the server parses it strictly and reads the title and poster once on save), Picture (one picture with its description required, a caption and a link), Gallery (two to twelve pictures picked several at once, each described, moved up or removed, as a swipe row or a grid), Social icons (the person's own social links as a row, nothing typed) and Banner (one line at the top, one live at a time). Each kind's limit disables its tile with the contract's sentence, as before.
- **Every block but a heading** carries a folded **Schedule** (`ScheduleFields` in `block-extras.tsx`): Show from and Hide after, each the field set's Date and time (`AttributeEditor` with a `timestamp` attribute), so it types and picks as every date in the CRM does, in the owner's time zone, stored as UTC. A block outside its window leaves the phone, as the public read leaves it out, and its row's chip says why ("Starts 3 Nov, 09:00", "Ends 10 Nov, 18:00", "Ended").
- **Sized kinds** (link, booking page, video, picture, gallery) take a **Size on a grid** (`SizePicker`, TileGrid's choice variant: Small, Wide, Large). A placed booking page block now has an editor of its own for its size and window (the menu's Edit; pressing its row still opens the booking page).
- **A link** gains a picture (uploaded, or "Use the site's picture": on an add it is fetched once the link is saved, since the server reads only the saved address; on an edit at once), Shown as Row or Featured card (a fourth featured link is refused on the field, AC-6102), and Ask first (Sensitive, 18+, 21+), the visitor's sentence shown under it.
- **Pictures** pass through the library's cropper first (spec 0068, AC-6809: `usePictureUpload` in `block-extras.tsx` opens `PhotoCrop`, free for an image block, a link's picture and each gallery picture in turn, square for a QR code's centre; "Adjust crop" crops the one there again) and are drawn again in the browser (`preparePhoto(source, 'picture', crop)` from `@crm/ui/photo`: at most 1,600 pixels, a JPEG under 1.5 MB, a photo's location left behind) and uploaded through `data.linkPage` (`reach.uploadPicture`); the editor and the phone read them from the owner's own copy (`/api/profile/images/:id`), live or not.
- **Insights** (`Insights.tsx`), on the overview under Your page once the page holds something: 7, 30 or 90 days; views and visitors against the period before (Stats with their arrows), clicks and bookings; views per day as the library's lazy line chart (its Table view is the screen reader's path); the top values of each fact (Sites, Sources, Countries, Devices) as a Table; each block's clicks; the funnel from views to clicks to bookings with rates; Download CSV for the period. Empty: "No visits yet" and how to get some. Each period is one held read in the store, kept a minute.
- **Sharing** (`ShareTools.tsx`): Links for each place (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, an email signature, the QR) each marked with `utm_source` and `utm_medium`, and Your own, three checked words. Style the code: a colour (the AccentPicker, refused with its hint when it is too light to scan against the white ground), a picture in the middle (theirs or an upload), and a frame with a label; Save keeps it for the page, and the overview's code and Download code use it. PNG at 1024 and SVG, the centre picture written into the file.
- **Spec gap**: no page has a grid layout yet. Spec 0061 reads it from spec 0060's look, but neither spec stores one, so the sizes are kept and drawn only once a layout setting exists.

## Team pages (`/w/$slug/link-page?team=true`, TeamPagesScreen; spec 0070, slice 1)

- **Purpose**: everyone's link page in one place, for owners, admins and holders of `link_pages.manage_team` (AC-7001).
- **Main task**: open someone's page to edit it, claim a handle for someone with none, or hand out QR codes.
- **Leaves out**: adding people (Members), what a page holds (its own editor), Insights beyond this month's views.

One Table, as Members draws it: the person, their page's address, Live or Private, and views this calendar month (UTC), with a row menu (Edit page, Copy link, Open page, Download QR code as PNG or SVG; Claim link for a row with no page). Pressing a row opens that member's page; your own row opens your own. The bar holds Download all QR codes: every page's plain code (dark on light, the address alone, `https://<public origin>/<handle>`, so it keeps working whatever the page's look becomes) drawn out of sight by the library's QrCode and zipped in the browser (`team-qr.ts`, `@crm/contracts/zip`). Claim link is a Modal with the address field after the public host, started from the person's name and checked by the contract's rule before anything is sent; the page it makes starts Private. A Private page's code downloads too, on purpose: badges are printed before an event, and the code opens the page the moment it goes Live (its address never changes). One download runs at a time; the others wait, off, until it is saved. A member's page that can't be opened (they left, a stale address, an owner's page for someone who isn't an owner, or no permission) says so with the way back to Team pages. The list is read on each visit (`data.teamPages.list`); no change event carries anyone's page. The way in is a Team pages button on the person's own page's bar, shown only to those who may manage them; the server refuses everyone else (`FORBIDDEN`), and the screen says so.

**Add a page** (9 Nov 2026, AddPageDialog): the bar's primary button, for anyone who reached the list (the server checks `link_pages.manage_team` again, in Timefix and inside a partner's frame). A Modal with Name, Email (optional) and the link after the public host: the link follows the name until edited, and is checked 300 ms after the last key (`data.teamPages.checkHandle`; a spinner, then a tick and Free, or Taken with one free link from `suggestHandle` to pick). Create page makes a page only person (`data.teamPages.addPerson`): no login, no invitation, no email, a seat, and their page Private at that link. The new row joins the list in name order, and a toast offers Edit page. A page only person's row says Page only under the name, and its menu adds **Remove person** (a danger confirm: the link stops, the content is kept 30 days; `data.teamPages.removePerson`).

**A member's page** (`?member=<id>`, with `?edit` for the builder): the same LinkPageScreen on that member's own stores (`data.linkPage.loadFor(member)`, `data.brand.loadFor(member)`), every call naming `member` for the server's door (`enterTeamLinkPage`). Its title is "Priya's page" under Link page › Team pages. What is the account's own stays off it: social links, the rest of the Details card (the site, the closing line, sharing and search), the wallet pass, Bring a page across and Email signup. Opening it never puts the page live; only its switch does. Its Details card holds Name and Title only (9 November 2026): a page only person's change there (`data.teamPages.namePerson`, `link_pages.manage_team`, a `member.profile_updated` History row); anyone with a login, or a partner's person, shows them read only, since they keep their own. Every change is written to History as `link_page.changed_for` (Publish, Discard and Restore keep their own rows), and the member's own Your page card says "Edited by <name>" (`data.teamPages.editedBy`).
