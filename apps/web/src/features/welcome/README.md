# Welcome (first run)

Spec 0005, milestone 1 (AC-30), redesigned by spec 0036 (AC-1203 to AC-1214).

## `/welcome` (WelcomeScreen)

- **Purpose**: first run. A new person says who they are, names their workspace and may connect a calendar, watching the workspace take shape beside each step.
- **Main task**: Your name, Your workspace (Create workspace), Your booking page, Your link page, Connect your calendar, then Invite your team, into the workspace. Sign in and the code page come first in the same split card (the empty sketch, with no step count, `signInFrame` in `features/auth/AuthStates.tsx`); the count starts here at Step 1 of 6 (Step 1 of 5 where calendar sync is off).
- **No skipping** (the owner, 27 October 2026: "instead of letting the user skipping it"). Only Invite your team skips, and it is last. Your name, Your workspace, Your booking page and Your link page each have one primary and nothing else. Connect your calendar can't be forced, since the connecting happens at Google, so its way on says what it does ("Continue without a calendar") rather than offering a skip.
- **Leaves out**: a photo (file storage, #32), templates, and a questionnaire (taken out 22 October 2026); someone who already has a workspace is sent to it.

## The steps

- **Your name** (`AboutStep`, `?step=about`): "Your name" (Google's name, or empty) beside a round avatar whose initials follow it. Continue needs a name ("Enter your name, or skip this step."); Skip goes on without one. A member is never nameless: a skipped name is the account's, else one made from the email ("ada.lovelace@acme.com" gives "Ada Lovelace", `nameFromEmail`), else the email's first part (`memberNameFor`).
- **About you** (the questionnaire of role, team size and use) was taken out on 22 October 2026 (the owner: "remove the questionare screen", "reduce the overall friction"). An old `?step=details` link starts at Your name. Its answers are no longer asked or sent: the `member_onboarding` table and the contract's `onboarding` answers on workspace create stay in place unused (no migration in that change) and can be retired later. A draft kept from before still opens; its answers are dropped.
- **Your workspace** (`WorkspaceStep`): "Workspace name" follows your name ("Ada’s workspace") until edited, and "Web address" after a quiet "/w/" follows the workspace name until typed or picked (`useAddress`): the first free one of `addressCandidates`, best first (the name without "’s workspace", so "Stealth’s workspace" gives stealth; then a company email's domain, stealth.design giving stealth and stealth-design, never a personal mailbox's; then the whole name, then -team, -hq and -app; a number only last). Typed, the address turns lowercase and spaces become dashes (`addressAsTyped`). Each address is checked once through `data.workspaces.slugAvailable`, 300 ms after the last key, at most six checks a change: a spinner and "Checking…", then a tick and "Available", or "Taken" on the field; the settled answer is announced. When the typed one is taken, or the first choice was, up to three free ones show under the field as small Buttons (Field's `suggestions`, "Free addresses"); pressing one fills the field. An answer for an older name or address is dropped. A failed check (offline, too many) says "Not checked. Create will check it." and leaves Create to decide. Create workspace refuses a missing name and a broken or known taken address on their fields before asking; the server still has the last word (`SLUG_TAKEN` on the address).
- **Your booking page** (`BookingStep`, `?step=booking&workspace=<slug>`, 27 October 2026): the first page people open to book time. Two fields, "What are you offering?" (placeholder "Intro call") and one of four lengths (15, 30, 45, 60) as a SegmentedControl inside a FieldGroup carrying the line "It opens straight away, and you can change its hours and questions afterwards." Create booking page makes it through `data.bookingPages.create` with that one length and the member's default schedule (`scheduleId: null`), its id minted on the first try and sent again on every retry, so a lost answer leaves one page; `ID_TAKEN` takes a new one. The page joins the end of the link page by itself (spec 0051), which is why this step comes before Your link page: by the time its owner sees the page, there is something on it. Beside it, `BookingPagePreview` draws the booking page as a visitor will see it (BookingFrame with the host, the meeting as typed, its length as a fact, a month of this month's weekdays and a morning's sample times), inert and `aria-hidden`, since the frame draws a `main` of its own and the page already has one. The hours aren't set yet, so the times are a sketch, not an offer. No skip.
- **Your link page** (`HandleStep`, `?step=handle&workspace=<slug>`, 27 October 2026): the address the link page and every booking page under it start with. One field after a quiet "timefix.com/" (`publicBookingHost`), checked against the contract's own `handleProblem` before anything is sent, then Claim your link page (`data.profile.update({ handle })`); the server has the last word, and a refusal lands on the field through `refusals.ts`. A handle is made with every membership (26 October 2026), so the field opens on the one they were given (`suggestedHandle`, else the claimed one) and the step is where they take it or change it — which is why it is always asked, even for a second workspace. Beside it, `LinkPagePreview` draws their own page: the person, the address as it is typed, and the booking page just made as its one row. No skip.
- **Invite your team** (`InviteStep`, `?step=invite&workspace=<slug>`, once the workspace exists) (the owner, 27 October 2026: the "Your link page is live" Callout came out, since the phone beside the step already shows the page live with its address to copy, and the Callout sat between the invite form's fields and its button as if it were part of inviting). It is the one invite form Settings › Members' dialog uses (`useInvite` with the library's `InviteRows`): a row per person, "+ Add another" (or Enter) for the next, a pasted list splitting into rows, and each row's role where the route could read the roles its maker may invite as (`roles.list` and `access.mine` in the loader; every row starts as Member). Unread, there is no role column and everyone joins as a member. Invite ("Invite 2 people", the dialog's words) sends one call per role (`data.invitations.create`), then says how many went out, any already in or not mailed, and Continue. Skip for now goes on. No Back: the workspace exists.
- **Connect your calendar** (`CalendarStep`, only where `system.status` says `calendarSync`): Connect Google Calendar leaves for the API's connect start with `then=welcome`, which comes back to `/welcome?step=calendar&workspace=<slug>&calendar=<result>`; the result shows as a callout (connected, then Continue; or why not, with Connect again). Connect Outlook is off, and the step's description ends "Outlook is coming soon.". Continue without a calendar goes on to Invite your team. Invite your team is the last step either way.

## Notes

- The step is in the address (`?step=about|workspace`), so the browser's back button moves between steps; Back (an icon button above the title, from the second step) goes back through the same history when it came from Continue, and keeps what was typed. Once the workspace is made, the calendar step has no Back: going back would mean renaming the workspace. The route keeps the calendar step for after the workspace exists (`?workspace=<slug>`, one of the person's own), and sends anyone else with a workspace to it.
- The page is AuthLayout's split screen (full screen since 22 October 2026): the step's panel on the left with the mark at its top, and `WorkspacePreview` filling the right from 1024 px up (the workspace's initials tile and name, the person's avatar and name, the part the step is about ringed), "Step N of M" (Steps' `count`) above the title, and "Signed in as {email}" with Sign out at the panel's foot. Sign out asks first (`features/auth/SignOutDialog.tsx`, the owner's rule of 21 October 2026): "Sign out?", Cancel (ESC) first, a primary Sign out last that spins while it runs and runs once.
- What was typed is kept in this tab's sessionStorage for the signed in person only (`draft.ts`), with the workspace's id, a UUID v7 minted once (`data.workspaces.newId()`) and sent again on every try, so a reload keeps the steps and a retry after a lost answer makes nothing twice. Making the workspace, or signing out, forgets it.
- Refusals land on their fields through Form's `fieldFor` (`refusals.ts`): `SLUG_TAKEN` on the web address (the API names the field), input problems by their path, anything else above the fields.
- A step change keeps the page, so the screen moves focus itself: the new step's first field (the calendar step's first working button). The first step shown is the route's to focus (`features/navigation/focus.ts`).
- Making the workspace loads the calendar step before showing it, so the card goes straight there; with calendar sync off, it opens People.
