# Bookings

A sidebar destination after Records, as Timefix's rail has it.

## Bookings (`/w/$slug/bookings`, the layout, as Captures)

- The frame, the TopBar (`BookingsTopBar`: the scheduling tile, the upcoming count, and New, named New meeting, which opens New meeting at the next free half hour of the week on screen inside the member's hours, else of the next two weeks, each read only once the one before is full (`findFreeSpan`; off the Calendar tab the week on screen and the member's hours are read first too, New busy meanwhile; a week whose read failed ends the look and is read again on the next press), and only then the next half hour from now, `free.ts`; off with the reason until a booking page is on) and the bar **Calendar · List · Roster · Availability · Round robin · Calendars** (`BookingsTabs`, Tabs' `bar` variant in the row People's ViewBar takes, `tabs.ts`; spec 0037, AC-1447), each tab its own route drawn in the chosen panel. Roster (`bookings.roster`, the Team week, `?week=` and `?q=`) shows only to an admin or someone whose scheduling reach sees a colleague (`rosterIsShown` in `booking/roster-load.ts`); its address falls back to Availability for anyone else, and an old `availability?view=team` link goes on to it. The layout owns New, so it keeps one place and one width on every tab.
- The tab's tools sit at the strip's end (`toolbarSlot.ts`: the screen portals them there), as Captures' search and New do.
- Bare `/bookings` goes to Calendar; an old `?view=list` link goes to List, keeping the rest of its address.
- Availability is `bookings.availability`, unchanged: its own WorkspacePage draws only its body inside the layout's frame (`WorkspacePage` knows when it is nested).

## New meeting (`NewMeetingDialog`)

- Without a calendar connected, the note that the meeting won't go on their calendar and a video call gets no link (the guest is still emailed, with the invitation attached) comes first, above the fields, so it is read before they are filled in (it sat under the fold).
- With (their name), Email and Their time zone together, since the zone only sets how their invitation reads the time; then Day (a DatePicker, changed in place: the day used to be read only with "close this and pick a time on the calendar", a dead end; Calendly and Basecamp change the day in their new event dialogs), From and To with the member's zone under them, the booking page, Calendar, Also invite and Message. A day outside the week on screen moves the week there, so the drawn span and its ghost stay in view. Their time zone says it only changes how their invitation shows the time.
- **With finds people as you type** (spec 0027's follow up, `people.ts`): the record picker's search over People (`records.search`, which now answers each record's first email the member may see), shown under the field as the library's SuggestionList (Field's `combobox`, the comment composer's pattern: focus stays in With). Each row is the person's avatar, name and email; someone with no email isn't offered, since they can't be invited. Nothing is highlighted until Down or Up moves to a row, so Enter alone still books; Enter or Tab on a row (or a click) fills With and Email (Tab going on to Email), Esc or leaving the field puts the list away, and typing on brings it back. Someone new is just typed, as before: the server files the booking to the person with that email, or makes one. Without access to People, With is a plain name.
- **Also invite** is the field set's Email editor with several values: each address a removable chip under the field's name, Enter adding the one typed (never booking on its way), up to 10 ("Up to 10 guests."). It holds still while Book it is out, as Day, the times and Calendar do.
- **Calendar**, only with two or more to choose between (spec 0024 milestone 5): the calendars in use that take events (`isCalendarInUse`, below) and the page's own "Add events to" even when it isn't in use, each in its colour, an account with two or more under its address. It starts on the page's own when that is one of the member's, else their default, which is where the event goes with no choice made; only a pick is sent (`calendarId`), and the server refuses one that isn't theirs to write on a working account.
- **Meeting limits** (spec 0037, job J7's follow up): booking in skips the host's meeting limits, as it skips their hours, so it only warns. Once the time and the host (Me or For) rest a quarter second, `limitsAt` (`data.bookings.limitsAt`, the `limits.at` read: the caller or a colleague they Manage, the same reach as booking in; each limit only when set) says how many meetings the host already has that day and week, and at or past a limit a warning Callout, announced, sits after For: "Sam is at their limit of 5 meetings that day." Book it still books. A failed read shows nothing.

## Calendar (`/w/$slug/bookings/calendar`, BookingsCalendarScreen)

- **Purpose**: the week: bookings beside the member's own calendar events.
- **Main task**: see the week (or one day) at a glance and open a booking or an event for its details.
- **Leaves out**: filing, and live arrival; the team's day is the Team view (below).
- The week's own controls bar (SectionBar `bar`, under the tabs, over the days; the owner, 22 October 2026): Today ("Today · 19 Oct" off this week), previous and next week (a day in the day view), the range as the bar's title (strong, 30 October 2026; the day's name in the day view), then the calendar switch (`CalendarSwitch.tsx`: a fitted SegmentedControl of All calendars, then each connected calendar on the week by its name with its colour dot, each segment as wide as its name, a name past `size-column` cut with the whole in a tooltip; past five segments the rest fold into a More menu, and one chosen from there takes the last segment), and Filter at the end. The switch is only for the connected calendars (the owner, 22 October 2026): bookings always show, and a calendar picked filters only the calendars' events and busy time; closed time always shows. With one calendar or none it is hidden, since there is nothing to choose. The choice is kept per workspace in this browser (storage blocked: All calendars until reload); a kept calendar that left the week, or one kept from the old Bookings segment, reads as All calendars. With no calendar connected, Connect calendar sits there instead. The calendars panel stays folded on the week, since the switch replaces its boxes (the List tab keeps it). Day and Week (a compact SegmentedControl, kept in the address as `v=day`, so a reload or a shared link keeps it) sit before Filter: Day is the picked day across the width (WeekCalendar `view="day"`). Single keys, as Google Calendar's (`week-keys.ts`): T today, J next, K previous, D day, W week, never while typing or inside a dialog or menu; Today and the arrows teach theirs in their tooltips (Tooltip `kbd`, which also sets each button's `aria-keyshortcuts`), Day and Week state theirs on their segments (`kbd`), and the shortcut help (?) lists all five in a Calendar group while this tab is open.
- Bookings take their link's colour (`linkHue`, the Links card's edge) and Timefix's mark before the title (the owner, 31 October 2026: what came through Timefix shows at a glance; a synced event carries its calendar's mark instead, and Busy none; the block's name says "Timefix booking"), and show a status only when it is news (No show, Needs a host; cancelled ones struck through). A dialog opened from a booking's card (Reschedule, Cancel booking, Reassign) closes the card first, so it opens alone, on top, with focus in it; dismissed, the card opens again with focus on the button that asked.
- The library's WeekCalendar, flush with the page (PageColumn `flush`, no card and no gap, the owner's ask of 22 October 2026): blocks by time, a booking's title and time with the guest on a third line, events quieter in their calendar's hue; today's date the link colour in a hairline ring (the one today mark) with the now line in its column; no all day events (the owner, 22 October 2026: they stay in the List tab); the hours outside the member's availability shaded (`openHoursOf`: the route's `bookings.hours` read, which it already makes, so no new request; nothing shaded when those hours are kept in another zone). It opens on the hour before now, else at the start of the member's day. Pressing a block opens its card beside it. An empty week says "No meetings this week" quietly in the grid, with "Next booking: Tue 3 Nov" under it when the reader has a confirmed booking after it (the upcoming bookings list, `meetings` upcoming, read only while the week is empty, through `NextBookingProbe`); pressing it goes to that day's week, and focus lands on that day. In the day view the line speaks of the day ("No meetings this day") and shows while the day shown is empty. The count is for screen readers. At phone width, the picked day under the seven day buttons.
- **The member's zone** (Settings › Account › Preferences, read through the workspace frame's FormatScope, `useFormatSettings().timeZone`; the browser's only when they have none): the week's days and its read range, each block's day and hour, the now line and today's date, the drawn span and its times, closed time made from it, New meeting's start (`nextFreeSpan`), the List tab's day bands and times, and Reschedule's slot list all read instants on that clock (`week.ts`: `dayKeyOf`, `minuteOfDay`, `instantAt`, `weekRange`, `piecesOf`, each taking the zone). Rows are the clock's hours, so on a daylight saving day a meeting sits at its clock time. The loaders read the same zone (`memberZone`), so their held week is the one the screen holds. The corner over the hour axis names the zone ("GMT+5:30", read out as "Times in Kolkata time (GMT+5:30)"), since it may not be the device's clock, with the offset of the week on screen. New meeting's From and To say which zone they are in; the List names it to screen readers. All day events stay by their dates.
- The address: `w` the week's Monday, `d` the picked day, `link` one booking page, `cal` the calendars the panel shows.

## Team day (`/w/$slug/bookings/calendar?v=team`, TeamDayView)

- **Purpose**: the team's day side by side (spec 0030, as spec 0037 amends it): who is free, booked or away, what needs a host and what clashes, for everyone the reader may See (`members.visible`'s rule, AC-1434).
- **Main task**: spot what needs doing and act on a teammate's column: Book someone in, Block time and Time off from a span drawn on it, Reassign from a booking's card, for people the reader Manages.
- **Leaves out**: changing working hours (Availability's Team week), dragging a booking to another column (Reassign from its card, AC-777), colleagues' calendar details (Busy only, AC-770), and a month or week of the team.
- **Where**: Team beside Day and Week on the calendar's bar (`v=team` in the address; spec 0030 wrote `view=team` before the bar had `v`). The steppers move a day; Today, Go to date and the keys work as in Day. The layout's Mine · Everyone steps aside here: the Team day is everyone the reader may see.
- **Grid**: the library's TeamDay (WeekCalendar's `team` view): one column per person, at least `size-column` (200px) and never squeezed to fit, scrolling sideways (snapping to a column's edge) under a pinned head and hour axis; each head is the person's picture and name over one short line, the status (Free in green, Off quieter, Away in yellow, N booked quiet) and their zone's offset when it differs; the job title (AC-1441, set in Profile) in the head's tooltip and the column's name (the owner, 31 October 2026: "show less"), and on the line while Filter's order is "By job title" (AC-1438), so the order explains itself; their calendar's health chip only when it is trouble (Reconnect, Some calendars skipped, AC-1307), never for no calendar. Open hours light, the rest sunken; time off a quiet yellow wash with one "Away · Vacation" chip pinned at its top (the reason for Manage only, AC-1437, AC-1286). Bookings in their link's colour with Timefix's mark, with "Clash" or "Outside hours" when that is news (Needs a host and Host away say so once, in the strip); what overlaps in a column sits side by side, so a Busy never covers a booking; blocks hatched (labelled for Manage only, AC-772); colleagues' calendars as Busy; the reader's own events in full, with their calendar's mark.
- **Acting**: empty time on a column the reader Manages (or their own) draws a span: Book someone in (New meeting, For set to that person, their pages), Block this time (For set to them), Time off (TimeOffDialog for them). A column they may only See shows no crosshair and draws nothing. The server checks every write again (AC-775).
- **Needs a host** (AC-1435): one slim strip over the grid, under the bar (the owner, 31 October 2026: "everything on left needs to be on top side", "the calendar shouldn't get small"), only when something is in it and only for people who Manage someone (or `booking.admin`): "N need a host" (Button `warning`), which lists them all, then one chip a booking (time, title, guest, and Host away when it is that), scrolling sideways when there are many, each opening its card under it with Reassign; a card opened from the list goes back to the list. On a phone the chips give way to the one button, and its list and cards are sheets. The day's issues ("2 issues") sit in the bar in the same warning colour (Button `warning`).
- **Issues** (AC-1436): "2 issues" in the bar (only when there are any), a menu with Outside their hours and Clashes, each by time, page and person; choosing one focuses its block (Enter opens its card).
- **Filter**: Order (Me first, then by name; By job title), Only people with bookings, and People (everyone the reader may see, or some), all in the address (`order`, `booked`, `who`). Past 50 people the day says "Showing 50 of 64. Filter to see the rest."
- **Empty**: fewer than two people (AC-764): "A team calendar needs a team", with Invite members for those who may invite; filters that match nobody offer Clear filters.
- **Data**: `data.bookings.teamDay(workspace, { day, timeZone, memberIds, withBookings, order })` (`useTeamDay`), one `bookings.teamDay` read per day and filter, kept current as the week is (`fresh.ts`) and primed for the days either side; a block drawn there and a reassign show at once (`bookings/team-day.ts`), put back on a refusal.

## List (`/w/$slug/bookings/list`, BookingsListScreen)

- **Purpose**: every meeting, Upcoming, Past or Cancelled: the bookings the member may see and their own synced calendar events, as the week shows them (the owner's ask of 21 October 2026).
- **Main task**: read what is coming up and open one for its details.
- **Leaves out**: colleagues' calendar events (their time is Busy on the week only, spec 0037), live arrival.
- The strip: Upcoming · Past · Cancelled, then the Calendars toggle and Filter, which holds Show (Everything, Bookings only, Calendar events only; only with a calendar connected, since without one there are only bookings) and, with two or more links, one link (the owner, 22 October 2026: two segmented controls side by side were too heavy). The calendars panel beside the list picks the calendars whose events show, as on the week, so it stays here; it is the same slim panel without the month.
- Rows (the library's BookingList) under day bands that stick while the list scrolls (Today, Tomorrow, the date): each row its time (the band says the day; All day for an all day event, read by its date), then a booking titled by its page with its status tag (Confirmed, Cancelled, Needs a host, No show) and "You and <guest>", the guest a link to their person as in round 6, and its other guests; a calendar event by its title, with its calendar's colour and name and its attendees. A row opens its card in a Panel.
- Pages of 50 by cursor (`bookings.meetings`, `data.bookings.meetings`, `useMeetings`); Show more reads the next. Each tab's empty state is the library's EmptyState centred in the list area (`layout="fill"`) with one line of help; Upcoming adds Share a booking page (to the booking links) and, with no calendar connected, Connect a calendar. Filtered, it says nothing matches, with Clear filters.
- The address: `tab` (`past`, `cancelled`), `src` (`bookings`, `calendar`), `link`, `cal`.
- The TopBar has no count: the list pages every meeting, so there is no total to show.

## A booking's card (both tabs)

- The library's MeetingCard (22 October 2026, the owner's "fix this UI", after Google Calendar's, Calendly's and Cal.com's event cards): the heading is who and which page ("Mateo García · Product demo") at the title size, its time under it with the status a quiet dot beside the time (Confirmed, Cancelled, Needs a host, No show). Then icon rows on one rhythm: the guest with their avatar, their name linking to their person and their email under it with a copy button (never a mailto link), any other guests; where, by its provider ("Google Meet") or the place, never the raw address; the note; "By chat on the booking page" with View chat (N) opening the transcript under it; why it was cancelled; the round robin's assignment. Notices (the calendar event couldn't be made, it needs a host, its host was removed) sit under the heading. The footer is one row, as Google Calendar's and Cal.com's: Join (primary, while the call can be joined; no primary without a call), Reschedule, then More (…) holding Copy guest link, Reassign for an admin, and Cancel booking in the danger tone, still asking first, with focus back on More when its confirm is dismissed; once started, Mark no show. Calendar events, busy time and closed time use the same card (Join for an event with a call; Remove or Release at the end for closed time).
- On the week the card is a popover beside the block, flipping to stay on screen; on a phone a sheet at the window's foot (Modal `sheet`). On the List it fills the side panel.
- While a booking is ahead, its card ends with **Reschedule** (the page's free times for the next two weeks, days over times, and a commit that names the new time; times on the member's clock with no 12h or 24h switch, which is the visitor's on the public page), **Copy guest link** (read only when pressed: it is the guest's key) and **Cancel booking** (asks first, naming the booking, with an optional reason the guest's email carries; the confirm stays busy until the server answers and keeps a refusal), in a row (`BookingActions.tsx`, `hostActions.ts`, spec 0025 AC-640). A change shows at once in every list and range, says so in a toast, and is undone on a refusal. On the week the card is a popover, which React Aria draws above any dialog, so Reschedule and Cancel booking close the card and the week draws the dialog itself (`BookingDialog`); Cancel or Esc opens the card again on the button that asked, and once the change goes through focus goes to its day. A closed time's Remove (Release for a hold, Remove every <weekday> for a weekly block) asks first the same way: "Remove the “Dentist” block?", what reopens, and that it can't be undone.
- **Link to a record** (polish F2, `BookingActions.tsx`, `hostActions.ts`, `data.bookings.link`): More holds Link to a record (Change linked record once linked, and Unlink record), past or ahead, for the objects the member may change but People and Companies (the guest's, filed when it was made), Deals first. Its dialog is the field set's record picker with the object switcher; with nothing linked yet its search opens at once, and it holds still while Link is out. The card then shows a Linked to row with the record's chip, opening its page, and a × that unlinks at once with Undo (focus goes to More). Unlink is the one word for taking it off: More's item, the ×'s name and the toast. The link shows at once in every list and range and is put back on a refusal. A record the member can't see (a hidden object, a record rule), one in the trash or on an archived object is absent, so the card shows no link; they may still link another, which replaces it, since refusing would say one is there.

## Notes

- Laid out after the reference product's bookings page; the look is ours (tokens, `@crm/ui`), the spacing Captures' (`PageColumn`, full width like every page).
- **Calendars in use only** (the owner, 23 October 2026: the others "don't show this anywhere"): the week's switch, the calendars panel and New meeting's Calendar list only an account's calendars in use (`isCalendarInUse` in `../calendars/in-use.ts`: the main calendar, one with Check for conflicts, Show on my week or Log meetings on, or where bookings go). The others Google lists (subscribed, shared, read only) never show; the switch and the panel list only calendars on the week, which are all in use.
- **Calendars panel** (`CalendarsAside.tsx`, the library's AsideLayout and CalendarList): beside the list, as Google Calendar keeps it, a menu's width (`size-menu`, narrower than the sidebar). Then "My calendars": an account with one calendar is that calendar's row alone, with its provider's mark; two or more fold under the account's mark and address. Each calendar the view shows is a checkbox filled with its colour, picking what this view shows (`cal` in the address, never the calendar's own switches, which stay in Settings › Calendars). Each row's options (Show only this) show on hover or focus. Which calendar takes new bookings is a setting, so neither Default nor Make default is here; Settings › Calendars owns it. Show all follows the list while any is hidden. When the default moved off a stopped or removed calendar, a notice names both, with Dismiss. No calendar connected: one line linking to Settings › Calendars. The List bar's Calendars button folds the panel (kept per browser); on a phone the panel is a sheet it opens. On the week the panel stays folded: the switch in the week's bar picks the calendar, Go to date (a calendar icon after the arrows) opens the small month (MonthCalendar in a Popover, the week on screen tinted, `shownWeek`), and a failed calendars read shows Try again in the switch's place. The default moved notice shows on the List tab.
- **Mine · Everyone** (`HostsSwitch.tsx`; the owner's ask of 29 October 2026, a colleague's booking stays theirs): an owner or admin (`booking.admin`) has a compact SegmentedControl at the very end of the Calendar · List strip, after the tab's own tools, on Calendar and List (one switch, drawn by the Bookings layout with the choice in context, `BookingsHosts` in `toolbarSlot.ts`, so it stands still as the tabs swap their tools in beside it: one in each tab's own bar moved with them, a layout shift the switching flow caught), Mine (their own pages', hosted and co-hosted bookings, the default) or Everyone. Anyone else sees only their own, so nothing shows for them. The choice is kept per workspace in this browser (storage blocked: Mine until reload); the loaders wait for Mine, the default, and never read it, so it stays out of the first load (an admin who kept Everyone sees the week or list load once more) (`hosts` on `bookings.calendar` and `bookings.meetings`). On Everyone a colleague's booking names its host: on its week block after the guest, and as a Host row on its card. An admin's empty Mine adds nothing to its empty state: the switch sits right above it, and another action there shifted the layout.
- **Filter** is one link's bookings, with two or more links; filtered it reads "Filter · 1", with a quiet clear beside it.
- **Colours**: each event in its calendar's hue (the member's pick, else the provider's colour's nearest); before the list reads, its account's.
- **Calendar events**: calendar sync stores meetings with someone else (titles, times, links), not events the member is alone in, which sync skips (spec 0024, AC-605). Only the member's own connections' events show. A booking's own event is left out: one marked as the booking's, or synced back with the booking's host, start and end and its video link or visitor.
- **Data**: `data.bookings.view(workspace, tab)`, `data.bookings.calendar(workspace, from, to)` and `data.bookings.meetings(workspace, query)` (`useBookings`, `useBookingsCalendar`, `useMeetings`), each read again whenever the window gets focus or the tab is shown again and, while live updates are paused or off, every minute while the page shows (owner ask A115: bookings and synced meetings write no change event Bookings hears, so new meetings show by themselves; the data layer's `bookings/fresh.ts`).
