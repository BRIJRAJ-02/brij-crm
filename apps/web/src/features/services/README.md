# Services, staff and shifts

The settings screens of the visit engine (spec 0066 B1), for a salon, a clinic or a studio setting up from an empty
workspace. Data comes only from `data.services` (`packages/data/src/services/store.ts`): every change shows at once,
goes back with the server's reason when refused, and every write's `visits` change event reads the held copies again
in every open tab. Nothing here calls the network.

| Screen                   | Brief                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `ServicesScreen.tsx`     | Settings › Services (AC-6610, AC-6613): the setup checklist, then the categories as sections, each a Table of its services (length, price, who does it, an Online switch), drag (or Move up and down) to order, select several for Move to, Online, Offline or Archive (Undo), archived services under a fold with Restore. An empty catalogue has one next action, the starter for the business's kind ("Add Haircut", made in a Hair category). Categories: New category, Rename, Move up and down, Delete (asks first; its services stay under Other services). |
| `ServiceDrawer.tsx`      | One service in a drawer: name, category, description, options (length, time before, how the price reads and the amount; one option takes the service's name), places (every place to start), who does it (everyone, or chosen people), what it needs (any of a kind, or a named room or chair, at those places), time after, online, a cancellation note. Asks before throwing typed changes away.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `StaffScreen.tsx`        | Settings › Staff (AC-6611): the setup checklist, then people as rows (photo or their colour, title, places, services, sign in), drag to order (the order "Any professional" breaks ties by), Archive asking first (refused with the count while visits are to come). Add staff asks only a name and a title: they start at every place on its opening week, doing every service, and their profile opens.                                                                                                                                                          |
| `StaffProfileScreen.tsx` | One profile: photo (the shared round cropper, `usePhotoCrop` and `preparePhoto(source, 'avatar', crop)`, uploaded at once), name, title, colour, bio, places and services with a SaveBar; the member link (the engine's `linkStaffMember`, its refusal under the field); each place's regular week (`RegularShifts`); time off and blocks to come; Archive.                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `ShiftsScreen.tsx`       | Settings › Shifts (AC-6612; Fresha's Scheduled shifts): the library TeamWeek for one place and week (`?place=&week=`), photos and colours on the rows, time off as Away; a cell opens one date's hours (HoursEditor `isSingleDay`, Back to regular); a row's menu sets the regular week, copies this week to all weeks (one write with all seven days, `copyWeekToAll`), copies shifts to others, adds time off or blocks time. `services.calendar` is enough: the desk sets the rota.                                                                             |
| `RegularShifts.tsx`      | A regular week: HoursEditor rows saved as they change, one write however many days Copy times to or Set all days touched.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `TimeDialogs.tsx`        | Time off (whole days on the place's clock, a reason, one place or all) and a block (a day and its hours). Linked staff have neither: theirs come from their own Availability.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `SetupSteps.tsx`         | The Appointments checklist (spec 0068, Foolproof): business and place, hours, a service, its people, their shifts, Publish. Hide setup keeps it away in this browser.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `shifts.ts`              | The pure rules the grid and the tests share: a date's own change, back to regular, a week copied to every week.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
