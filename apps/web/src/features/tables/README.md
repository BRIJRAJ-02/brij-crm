# Table seating (spec 0066 B7 lite)

The staff side of table reservations: one page, Reservations, in the Hospitality section while Table seating is installed, laid out after checklabs' Reservations page (5 November 2026). It reads through `data.tables` (the setup and each day) and `data.hospitality` (the places and their tables); nothing here calls the network.

| Screen                   | Brief                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `ReservationsScreen.tsx` | Run the day (`/w/$slug/reservations`, `?date=` the day shown, today left out; Service's old address redirects here with its day). The bar: the date (a short day that opens a month to pick any other, on a phone too), then Previous, Today and Next, List or Floor, Online booking (for those who set the apps up), Walk in (today only) and New booking; a narrower bar keeps the date, New booking and what fits, the rest in More. Checklabs' day calendar: the chosen day alone (WeekCalendar's `day` view given one day), drawn 10:00 to midnight or from the day's first service or booking when sooner (`day-hours.ts`), cancelled and no shows left out, a picked time opening New booking at it; its line counts the bookings and the covers (booked, seated, to come). The bookings list under it (over it on a phone): time, the guest with their phone under the name, guests, table, the source as a badge (Phone, Walk in, Online), the note, the status, and the row's moves: Confirm inline while a booking isn't confirmed, then Seat or Finish, No show and Cancel in its menu (each asking first), Undo for 10 seconds; "No bookings on …" on an empty day. A block or a row opens the booking's detail (`NewBooking.tsx`'s `VisitSheet`). The floor: table tiles by area (free, booked soon, seated with its timer, needs attention 15 minutes past a booking). While guests can't reserve online, one line says so with Set up online booking. Live through the `visits` change event, and every minute where live updates are off. |
| `OnlineBooking.tsx`      | Checklabs' "Online booking" sheet (a drawer from the bar): the setup checklist (hours, tables, rules, Publish) until the page is live, then the page's link with Copy link and Open page; one form in checklabs' order and with its defaults (Accept online bookings; the place's time zone and service hours, shown with Edit links; slot interval 30 and turn time 90; min lead 120 minutes and booking window 60 days; min and max party 1 and 8; max covers per slot), then ours (turn time by party size, the guests' change and cancel cutoffs in minutes, new reservation emails); one Save settings at its foot, which publishes with Accept online bookings on, asks before taking the page offline, refuses a broken page with its one line fix and a link to it, and closes once saved. Closing with changes asks first.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `WalkIn.tsx`             | Seat a walk in, a drawer as New booking is: Guest name (optional), Party size (2 picked), the free tables that fit them now (best fit picked, or the table it was opened from), Seat now. Checklabs' waitlist waits for a later phase.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

`NewBooking.tsx` holds New booking (checklabs' sheet in its order: Guest name, Date, Time 19:00 or the time picked, Party size 2, Assign table offering only tables that seat the party or the best free one, Phone, Email, Notes; `tables.book`, source `desk`, which reads Phone) and the detail sheet.

The guest's side is `src/public/reserve/` (its own bundle, `reserve.html`).
