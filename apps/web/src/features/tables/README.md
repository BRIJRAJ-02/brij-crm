# Table seating (spec 0066 B7 lite)

The staff side of table reservations: one page, Reservations, in the Hospitality section while Table seating is installed, laid out after checklabs' Reservations page (5 November 2026). It reads through `data.tables` (the setup, each day, and the places and tables with only the fields it draws, so Run service alone can open it); nothing here calls the network.

| Screen                   | Brief                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `ReservationsScreen.tsx` | Run the day (`/w/$slug/reservations`; `?date=` the day shown, today left out, and `?place=` the place, the first left out; Service's old address redirects here with both). The bar: the date (a short day that opens a month to pick any other, on a phone too) with, on a desk's window (`bp-page-compact`), Previous and Next beside it and Today after them away from today, so nothing that comes and goes moves them; then List or Floor, Online booking (for those who set the apps up), Walk in (today only) and New booking. Narrower, the bar keeps the date, More (Today, the floor or the list, Online booking) and New booking, and Walk in sits by the bookings list. Checklabs' day calendar: the chosen day alone with no head (WeekCalendar's `day` view given one day), drawn 10:00 to midnight or from the day's first service or booking when sooner (`day-hours.ts`, `drawnHours`), eight hours at a time (`visibleHours`), cancelled and no shows left out, a picked time opening New booking at it; its line counts what is on it and the covers (booked, seated, to come). The bookings list under it (over it when narrower): time, the guest with their phone under the name, guests, table, the source as a badge (Phone, Walk in, Online), the note, the status, and the row's moves: Confirm inline while a booking isn't confirmed, then Seat or Finish (today only), No show (today) and Cancel reservation in its menu (each asking first), Undo for 10 seconds; narrower, the state and party go under the name and Confirm into the menu. A past day reads only; "No bookings on …" on an empty day. A block or a row opens the booking's detail (`NewBooking.tsx`'s `VisitSheet`). The floor: table tiles by area (free, booked soon, seated with its timer, needs attention 15 minutes past a booking). Until online booking was ever set up, one line says guests can't reserve online, with Set up online booking. The sheets stay mounted while they close, so they slide out. Live through the `visits` change event, and every minute where live updates are off. |
| `OnlineBooking.tsx` | Checklabs' "Online booking" sheet (a drawer from the bar): its top is the page's link to copy or open once live, else what stands in the way (the place's hours or tables, with Fix it) or how to publish; then one form in checklabs' order and with its defaults (Accept online bookings; the place's time zone and service hours, shown with Edit links; slot interval 30 and turn time 90; min lead 120 minutes and booking window 60 days; min and max party 1 and 8; max covers per slot), then ours (turn time by party size, the guests' change and cancel cutoffs in minutes, new reservation emails); one Save settings at its foot. Save sends only what was changed here over the latest copy (never undoing another manager's change), publishes with Accept online bookings on (the sheet then stays, on the link), asks before taking the page offline, refuses a broken page with its one line fix and a link to it, and closes once saved. Closing with changes asks first. A refused save keeps the form as typed, the switch included, so Fix it asks before leaving it. |
| `WalkIn.tsx` | Seat a walk in, a drawer as New booking is: Guest name (optional), Party size (2 picked), the free tables that fit them now (best fit picked, or the table it was opened from, each with its seats), Seat now; a name typed in isn't lost to a click outside. Checklabs' waitlist waits for a later phase. |

`NewBooking.tsx` holds New booking (checklabs' sheet in its order: Guest name, Date, Time 19:00 or the time picked, Party size 2, Assign table offering only tables that seat the party or the best free one, Phone, Email, Notes; `tables.book`, source `desk`, which reads Phone) and the detail sheet.

The guest's side is `src/public/reserve/` (its own bundle, `reserve.html`).
