# Forms

Captures, Forms (spec 0023, `0023-experience.md`): forms that collect submissions from a link or a site, and add each one to the CRM unless the form keeps them only. The routes are `/w/$slug/captures/forms` (the list) and `/w/$slug/captures/forms/$formId` (the editor: `?tab=` settings, install or submissions, `&state=` and `&submission=` on Submissions, `?question=<id>` to open one question); the Captures frame and its Links · Forms tabs come from the shell.

## Screens

- **The list** (`FormsScreen.tsx`): one LinkCard per form, the same card as Links (no colour mark: a form has no colour of its own): its submissions ("12 submissions · 2 spam", "No submissions yet") and its link, the Accepting switch ("<name> is live", its hint Live or Paused; turning it off pauses at once with Undo in the toast, as the editor's menu does), Copy link, Open and a menu (Duplicate, Delete, which names the submissions that go with it). A form never published shows its Not published badge and Edit, with no switch and no link yet. New form opens the template gallery (`TemplateGallery.tsx`, spec 0063, AC-6323; 5 November 2026, after Typeform's and Tally's galleries): a wide window listing the twelve templates by name and one line, Blank form first, picked and focused as it opens, with the picked one drawn beside the list as a visitor sees it (Modal's `aside`, the hosted page's own component in a phone; left out under 1024px, where the list alone picks); the arrows browse, and Use <name> makes the draft and opens it. Blank is truly empty and opens on its first question, its words focused (`startsWithQuestion`); a form with no questions can't be published ("Add a question first."). Feedback and NPS are anonymous: they ask nobody who they are and keep their submissions only (core's `keepsSubmissionsOnly`), until Save answers to is turned on in Settings.
- **The editor** (`FormEditor.tsx`): the TopBar holds the name with its status beside it (EditorStatus: Live, Live · edited, Paused or Draft), then the Captures editors' shared header (`../captures/EditorActions.tsx`, the booking page editor's too): Copy link (from the first publish; a draft has no link yet, so it doesn't show, 22 October 2026), no Insights (T22: the Analytics tab is the one door), Preview (the draft in a new tab, marked Preview, not live), Publish, the page's one primary, while there is something to publish (forms keep a draft; booking pages save directly and never show it), and the ⋯ menu: Pause or Resume (at once, a toast with Undo, 22 October 2026), Duplicate, Discard changes while the draft differs from the live form (asks first, since it loses work; the draft saved back to the live document is the live version again on the server, so the status reads Live and Publish goes), then Delete form, which asks first. The name changes in Settings, as a record's name changes in its fields, not from the header. PreviewLayout, the one Captures editor layout it shares with the booking page editor, holds the tabs once published (Form · Settings · Share · Submissions · Analytics), notices (a read only note, a refused publish, a teammate's change), and the live preview, drawn in a phone in the last third of the width (behind a Preview button that opens a sheet on a narrow window), with one Design button over it (5 November 2026: three pickers there read as clutter), a small panel holding Colour (Light or Dark), Corners (Rounded or Square) and Colours (Workspace brand or Plain), each a row of segments (spec 0063, AC-6327: Workspace brand wears the workspace's kit from Settings › Brand, which the preview draws through `FormBrand.tsx`; Plain the product's own. A workspace with no kit saved is told where to add one). The draft saves itself a second after the last change; a teammate's save first shows "Bea changed this form. Load their version".
- **Answer types** (spec 0063, AC-6307): beside the first twelve, Date (earliest and latest day), Time, Date and time, Rating, Scale (from 0 or 1 to 5, 7 or 10, with words for its ends), Multi select (least and most picks, and Other), Address and Hidden (a value when the link has none; never required). Each files onto the attribute types `FILES_ONTO` names; a Date and time or an Address has nothing Save could make for it beyond a date, a location or a timestamp field already there. A booking page's questions keep the first twelve (AC-6311). A form holds 50 questions.
- **Prefill** (`ShareTab.tsx`, AC-6309): For developers (a quiet sheet) opens with a Prefill link card: an example link to copy, and every question's key. `?key=value` fills a question; a key repeated fills a multi select's several picks; a hidden question takes it too. A value that doesn't fit is ignored.
- **The title and Layout** (critique c, blocker 2, 5 November 2026): the Form tab opens with the form's one title, the headline visitors read, in Field's `title` variant, and the line under it (optional), each edited in place (the preview's own press-to-edit stays). While the form is still Untitled form, leaving the title names the form after it; a template's draft whose headline nobody wrote takes the form's name once (`DEFAULT_HEADLINE`). After the first publish the private name lives in Settings. Layout (`LayoutCard.tsx`, spec 0063, AC-6302) is one segmented control, Classic, Steps or One at a time, beside the preview it changes. It lives in the draft and goes live with the next publish, so a visitor mid fill keeps the layout they started in. The preview takes two fifths of the width (`previewShare="wide"`, critique c, blocker 1), so a laptop at 1280 edits beside the phone rather than behind a Preview button.
- **Steps as pages** (`../questions/steps.ts`, spec 0063, AC-6303, AC-6312, AC-6313; T20, 5 November 2026, after Tally's page breaks): choosing Steps splits the form at once (`splitIntoSteps`): a header before the first question, titled Your details when a run of contact questions leads, and another after it (or in the middle); breaks the form already has are kept. With Steps each break is an inline header among the cards ("Step 2 · About you"), never a card; Classic and One at a time draw none, the breaks kept quietly. Between every two cards sits a quiet + (T39, 5 November 2026: adding in between "feels like a find"), always shown, to a pointer, the keyboard and touch alike: Add a question here (the types), and between two questions of one page Start a new step here too, which starts a page there with its title field open. Add a question here at the top of a page puts the question in that page. The Questions header has Add a step, which adds a page at the end holding one new question, its name typed first. Each header reads as a step: "Step 2 · About you", then how many questions it holds. A header's ⋯ has Rename (in place), Add logic (one click to a ready rule: When <its choice question> is <its first option>, Jump to <the next step>, or to an ending made for it), Show only if… and Merge with the step above (with Undo). A step's rules sit under its last question (critique c, 7: read after the questions they read), headed After this step, each "If … → …" with "Otherwise → Step 3" under them and + Add a rule; its Show only if stays under its header. The preview shows the page you pressed or are editing, with Step 1 of 3 and Next. A rule still being written is kept as a local draft and saves once it has its value. With One at a time each question is a screen of its own and carries its own rules (`question.jumps`, AC-6313): the question's ⋯ has Add logic (one click to a ready rule reading it, past the next question to the one after, or to a thank-you message, made if need be), and the rules sit right under the question as "After this question", each "If … → Question 4 · Budget" with "Otherwise → Question 3 · …" under them and + Add a rule; only the questions below it are offered (`questionJumpChoices`), and a renamed question's rules follow its new key. Publish refuses an empty step, a step's Show when over a later question, and a Show when on the step holding the email a submission is matched by.
- **Logic** (`FlowView.tsx`'s `LogicMap`, `logic.ts`; T41, 5 November 2026: "the rules need to be explicit and smart"): every rule reads as a sentence with a visible Otherwise ("If Budget is Under $1k → Thank-you message: Not a fit", "Otherwise → Step 3"; "Otherwise → this question is skipped" under a Show only if), values picked from the question's real choices, and a question's row carries a "Shown if …" chip. `logicProblems` (pure, unit tested) finds a rule that can never match, one reading a question that is gone, an earlier rule that always wins (the first match wins, and the card says which), a step nobody reaches (with One at a time, a question nobody reaches), a jump back up the form (ignored, never a loop) and a required question some people never see; each is said as a warning on the rule itself. A renamed choice is renamed in every rule (`followChoices`); removing a choice, or deleting a question, that rules read takes those rules with it under one Undo, said in the toast. The Questions header's "Logic · N" (a warning mark and "N to check" when something needs a look) opens the Logic map: what to check, each with Go to it; up to three one click suggestions (`logicSuggestions`: a different thank-you message for a choice, a top choice on to a booking page, low ratings to their own message); one card per step (or one for the questions) listing each Show only if and each rule, then where everyone else goes; After they send; and Test it, where answers picked from the real choices show the steps a visitor sees, the ones skipped and where they end (`testPath`, the same walk as the hosted page), nothing sent.
- **After they send** (`AfterSendSection.tsx`; T39, 5 November 2026, after Typeform's and Tally's logic in words): the end of the Form tab, read as sentences, under a plain heading with no header actions. Each rule is a Card holding one line, "If [Budget] [is] [Under $1k] → [A different thank-you message]" (`ConditionLines` from `../routing`, the Automations dialog's Sentence with inline Selects), its value control following the question (its real choices, yes or no, a number box, a short box, never "Set value…"), and the destination picked in the sentence: a different thank-you message (its heading, line and button under the line), a live booking page by name, or a link (its address under the line). The rule's ⋯ holds Add a condition, Move up, Move down (the first match wins) and Delete, with Undo. Then one quiet "+ Send some people somewhere else", which makes a ready rule on the first choice or yes or no question (never a consent, a name, an email or a phone; `ruleQuestion` in `logic.ts`), to a new thank-you message, and focuses its question. Then one Card: "Everyone sees [Thank you page]" ("Everyone else sees" once a rule exists), its words in a box under it; "[A booking page] [Intro call] [after 5 seconds]"; or "[A link]" with its address. A thank-you message only a step's jump reaches has its own Card saying which step sends people there. The word "ending" is never shown. Insert an answer puts `{{key}}` where the caret was, so nobody types braces. The Score line shows only once a question gives points, with where to save it. The document keeps its shapes: `after`, `endings`, `routing.rules`, `scoreFiledOn`.
- **Questions** (`../questions/QuestionsSection.tsx`, shared with a booking page's Questions tab since spec 0057; T40 and critique c, 5 November 2026): QuestionList cards, closed until opened, one open at a time. Add a question opens the answer types first, in a short grouped menu (Text, Choices and scores, Dates and places, More; a form's ready-made questions last), and puts the new question under the open one, its words focused. Choice is one type: Let people pick more than one and Show as a dropdown are its two switches (Multi select and Dropdown underneath); Checkbox stays only on a question that has it. An open card's head is the question itself, typed in place, large, with its type as a small icon menu at its start; Enter goes on to the next question of the same type, and Escape takes back a new question never given words. Its body holds only what the type needs (choices, a type's settings, points once given, the placeholder edited in place, Show only if once set). The footer is Required (a switch) and one ⋯: Show only if…, Save answer to (Keep with the answer first, then the fields it fits, then a new field, made only when picked), Use in Insights, Give points, Start a new step here, Duplicate and Delete with Undo. A new question keeps its answer with the submission, unless its words name a field the object has (Email, Company), which it then fills; making a field is explicit. A form that keeps its submissions only shows no Save answer to.
- **The first publish** (`FormEditor.tsx` `publish`, then `PublishDialog.tsx`'s `ReadyDialog`; spec 0058, AC-6; the owner, 5 November 2026, after Tally): one click, with nothing asked first. The form keeps its name (an untitled one goes live as Untitled form, renamed later in Settings), its link is made from the name when that is free, else it keeps its own key (`firstPublish` in `draft.ts`, a taken link falling back to the key), and each answer meets a field by its label (`fileByLabel`), the rest getting a field made for them (`createMissingAttributes`), the email made the match with a toast when an existing field becomes unique. Where answers go changes later in Settings › CRM. Then the live dialog (T44, 5 November 2026): one line, "<Form> is live" (the Modal's title), then `ShareBlock`: the link as ReadyPanel's `pill` (the host muted, the form's own name strong) with Copy link, the dialog's one primary, and a quiet Open; under it the QR code (QrCode's column) with PNG and SVG beside Email, WhatsApp and the device's share sheet; then one quiet link, Put it on your site, to the Share tab. Done, secondary, has focus, so Enter or Esc closes it.
- **Settings** (`SettingsTab.tsx`; T21, 5 November 2026, Linear's and Vercel's settings): plain rows, a label on the left and a compact control on the right, no card per section and no subtitles, a word of help in an InfoTip beside the label: Name and Link (with Copy) once published, Save answers to (the objects, then Only in Submissions, one picker for what was a switch and a Select), Owner (while answers go to the CRM; nobody, a teammate, or a round robin group; a problem is said under it), Email each submission to, Accepting submissions (Pause and Resume) and Spam (Review) once published. Each saves itself: the name and link as they are left, the rest as picked; where answers are saved goes live with Publish. The headline, line under it and button are edited on the Form tab's preview (press them), and everything after a visitor sends is After they send.
- **Share** (`ShareTab.tsx`, spec 0058, AC-11; T44, critique b5 to b10, S4, S7; called Install until 5 November 2026, `?tab=install` still opens it): row 1 is the live dialog's own `ShareBlock` in a Card (one way to share, in both places); row 2, Put it on your site: the three ways as tiles (`EMBED_TILES`, each saying what the visitor sees), then the choices beside `EmbedPreview`, a made up site showing the pick (In the page: Clear background and Hide the title switches, written as `data-timefix-ground` and `data-timefix-head`, which the embed script passes to the frame and the page reads, `embedOf` in `public/frame.ts`; Popup and Corner button: Button words; Corner button: Button colour), then the snippet they write, and the booking Callout. A form's snippet always names its Colour, light too, so the script's dialog matches the form inside it; the dialog's bar holds Close alone. For developers is one ghost button opening a `sheet` Modal: the prefill link, your own HTML, the allowed sites and signed posts.
- **Submissions** (`SubmissionsTab.tsx`): what needs a review on top, then a table (the person: their record, or the name they gave; when; the state, saying Not in CRM or Received for one with no record; their email; the form's next two questions), which scrolls sideways in its own frame when a long value widens it, When and State kept near the start, with search, a state filter, bulk Not spam, Add to CRM, Mark as spam and Delete (which asks first, since a submission cannot come back), export as CSV and Sync earlier submissions. A row opens the submission in a stacked Panel (22 October 2026, after Typeform's and Tally's response view and Attio's side panel): the header names who sent it (their record's name, the name answer, or the email) with the form and when under it, and the state as a StatusDot; then Answers, Records and Where it came from, each a FieldGroup headed as an h3, separated by hairlines, every row stacked label over value. Filing is said once after Answers ("3 of 9 saved on the record", `answer-filing.ts`), and the answers not saved are marked Not saved. A phone shows through the field set, spaced for reading; an email or a website stays plain text that wraps (a visitor typed it, so never a link), and all three copy their raw value from a button on hover or focus. Only the answers not saved are marked, the same group on every submission. While it is being added, Records says so in one quiet line. The footer has Delete at its start (danger, asking first in a Modal), and Mark as spam, Add to CRM or Not spam at its end; a failed one shows Retry with Add to CRM and Mark as spam under More.
- **Analytics** (`AnalyticsTab.tsx`, spec 0063, AC-6321; T45, 5 November 2026): the tab is the one door, drawn by the reusable `../insights/PageInsights.tsx` (a booking page's and the link page's next). The period (7, 30 or 90 days) sits at the end of the tab strip in every state. One row of figure cards (Stats' `cards`): Views, Starts, Finished, Completion and Median time, each against the period before only when there was something before. Where people stop is a Card whose line is the worst stop in one sentence ("Biggest drop: “Budget?”, 2 of the 7 who got there."), over a table of the questions by name with how many reached each and the share that stopped there; from five starts each row has a thin bar (Meter's `share`), under five only numbers. Views over time is a line once there are five views over more than one day, its axis in whole numbers. Where they come from is a short table. With no visits at all, the empty state offers Share the form. Counting: a look is not a start, and a question is reached only once the visitor has answered something (Classic by the furthest answered question).
- **Sync earlier submissions** (`SyncDialog.tsx`, spec 0058, AC-14): offered once, right after a Publish that changed where answers are saved on a form that has submissions (never from the ⋯ menu; Submissions keeps its own door). One line, Only unsaved (chosen on open) or All as a compact SegmentedControl, the counts as Stats (only those that would happen: new records, updated, unchanged, can't be synced), and one button naming how many it would change, new records plus updates, never the ones that already match ("Sync 12 submissions"; every one in scope when the counts cover only the newest). When every one already matches it says "All 2 already match." with Done and no Sync; with none in scope it says Nothing to sync in one row and offers no button. It runs as a background job followed to its end (`followJobs.ts`).
- **Confirms** (`FormConfirms.tsx`, spec 0058, AC-18): Delete form, Rotate secret, Remove site and the object change each stay a confirm Modal with the question, one line under 12 words, and two buttons.
- **Empty list**: a still drawing of a form (`SpotIllustration`), "No forms yet", one line and New form, a secondary: the TopBar's New is the one primary.

## Rules

- `draft.ts` holds every change to a draft as a plain function; `draft.test.ts` checks them.
- The publish check is `publishProblem` from `@crm/data/forms`, the server's own.
- "Submission" is the one word for what a form collects, in every string here.
- The visitor's page is `src/public/main.ts`, drawn by `@crm/ui/public-form` with React and the library's own controls, under its budget in `apps/web/.size-limit.js` (223 kB of JavaScript and 17 kB of CSS gzipped). Steps and One at a time are their own lazy chunk, at most 10 kB, which a Classic form never loads (spec 0063).
