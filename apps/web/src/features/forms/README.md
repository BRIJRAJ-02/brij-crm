# Forms

Captures, Forms (spec 0023, `0023-experience.md`): forms that collect submissions from a link or a site, and add each one to the CRM unless the form keeps them only. The routes are `/w/$slug/captures/forms` (the list) and `/w/$slug/captures/forms/$formId` (the editor: `?tab=` settings, install or submissions, `&state=` and `&submission=` on Submissions, `?question=<id>` to open one question); the Captures frame and its Links · Forms tabs come from the shell.

## Screens

- **The list** (`FormsScreen.tsx`; lane 3c, 4 November 2026, after Klaviyo's forms list): one LinkCard per form, the same card as Links (no colour mark: a form has no colour of its own), its state as a badge (Draft, neutral, never a greyed title; Paused; New submissions) and one meta line ("5 questions · edited 2h ago · 4 submissions"; a draft has no submissions part), no address (a slug cut to "/f/booking-i…" said nothing; Copy link copies it whole). Its foot: Copy link, Submissions (in words: an inbox icon alone read as mail) and ⋯ (Open in a new tab, Pause, which asks first in a Modal since it ends something, or Resume, at once; Duplicate; Delete, which names the submissions that go with it). A reader without ⋯ keeps Open as an icon with its tooltip. No on/off switch: pausing from a card was one click with no question. A draft shows Edit. The sort at the bar's end is the record lists' SortChip: Updated (newest first, the default) or Name. New form opens the template gallery (`TemplateGallery.tsx`, spec 0063, AC-6323; 5 November 2026, after Typeform's and Tally's galleries): a wide window filled by a grid of the twelve templates, Blank form first, picked and focused as it opens, each card the form it makes drawn small (HostedFormPreview's `size="thumbnail"`, the hosted page's own component, at every width; forms fixes 3, after Miro's template grid) over its name and one line; the arrows browse, and Use <name>, Enter on a card or a double click (TileGrid's `onConfirm`) makes the draft and opens it. Blank's card is the form it makes, Untitled form with nothing asked. Blank is truly empty and opens on its first question, its words focused (`startsWithQuestion`); a form with no questions can't be published ("Add a question first."). Feedback and NPS are anonymous: they ask nobody who they are and keep their submissions only (core's `keepsSubmissionsOnly`), until Save answers to is turned on in Settings.
- **The editor** (`FormEditor.tsx`): the TopBar holds the name with its status beside it (EditorStatus: Live, Live · edited, Paused or Draft), then the Captures editors' shared header (`../captures/EditorActions.tsx`, the booking page editor's too): Copy link (from the first publish; a draft has no link yet, so it doesn't show, 22 October 2026), no Insights (T22: the Analytics tab is the one door), Preview (the draft in a new tab, marked Preview, not live), Publish, the page's one primary, while there is something to publish (forms keep a draft; booking pages save directly and never show it), and the ⋯ menu: Pause or Resume (at once, a toast with Undo, 22 October 2026), Duplicate, Discard changes while the draft differs from the live form (asks first, since it loses work; the draft saved back to the live document is the live version again on the server, so the status reads Live and Publish goes), then Delete form, which asks first. The name changes in Settings, as a record's name changes in its fields, not from the header. PreviewLayout, the one Captures editor layout it shares with the booking page editor, holds the tabs once published (Form · Settings · Share · Submissions · Analytics), notices (a read only note, a refused publish, a teammate's change), and the live preview, drawn in a phone in the last third of the width (behind a Preview button that opens a sheet on a narrow window), on a sunken canvas, the frame as tall as the form and centred (PreviewLayout's `previewSurface="canvas"`, forms fixes 3), a Phone or Desktop switch (icons) and the look over it, in view without a click (forms fixes 3, the owner: "upfront as it was earlier was better", after a Design popover hid it): three compact Selects of one height in the preview's head, Mode (Light or Dark), Corners (Rounded or Square) and Brand (Workspace brand or Plain) (`LookControls` in `FormBrand.tsx`; spec 0063, AC-6327: Workspace brand wears the workspace's kit from Settings › Brand, which the preview draws through `FormBrand.tsx`; Plain the product's own. A workspace with no kit saved is told where to add one). A status change made elsewhere (Pause in another tab, or the send that fills the form and pauses it, forms fixes 3) reaches the open editor live (`data.forms.onChanged`): the form is read again for its status, the draft being edited kept. The draft saves itself a second after the last change; a teammate's save first shows "Bea changed this form. Load their version".
- **Answer types** (spec 0063, AC-6307): beside the first twelve, Date (earliest and latest day), Time, Date and time, Rating, Scale (from 0 or 1 to 5, 7 or 10, with words for its ends), Multi select (least and most picks, and Other), Address and Hidden (a value when the link has none; never required). Each files onto the attribute types `FILES_ONTO` names; a Date and time or an Address has nothing Save could make for it beyond a date, a location or a timestamp field already there. A booking page's questions keep the first twelve (AC-6311). A form holds 50 questions.
- **Prefill** (`ShareTab.tsx`, AC-6309): For developers (a quiet sheet) opens with a Prefill link card: an example link to copy, and every question's key. `?key=value` fills a question; a key repeated fills a multi select's several picks; a hidden question takes it too. A value that doesn't fit is ignored.
- **The title and Layout** (critique c, blocker 2, 5 November 2026): the Form tab opens with the form's one title, the headline visitors read, in Field's `title` variant, and the line under it (optional) in the same quiet field one step down (`size="sm"`, forms fixes 3), each edited in place (the preview's own press-to-edit stays). While the form is still Untitled form, leaving the title names the form after it; a template's draft whose headline nobody wrote takes the form's name once (`DEFAULT_HEADLINE`). After the first publish the private name lives in Settings. Layout (`LayoutCard.tsx`, spec 0063, AC-6302) is one segmented control, Classic, Steps or One at a time, beside the preview it changes. It lives in the draft and goes live with the next publish, so a visitor mid fill keeps the layout they started in. The preview takes two fifths of the width (`previewShare="wide"`, critique c, blocker 1), so a laptop at 1280 edits beside the phone rather than behind a Preview button.
- **Steps as pages** (`../questions/steps.ts`, spec 0063, AC-6303, AC-6312, AC-6313; T20, 5 November 2026, after Tally's page breaks): choosing Steps splits the form at once (`splitIntoSteps`): a header before the first question, titled Your details when a run of contact questions leads, and another after it (or in the middle); breaks the form already has are kept. With Steps each break is an inline header among the cards ("Step 2 · About you"), never a card; Classic and One at a time draw none, the breaks kept quietly. Between two cards a quiet + shows while a pointer is over the gap or focus is in it (T39; forms fixes 3, the design critic: a + under every card was noise): Add a question here (the types), and between two questions of one page Start a new step here too, which starts a page there with its title field open. Add a question here at the top of a page puts the question in that page. The Questions header has Add a step, which adds a page at the end holding one new question, its name typed first. Each header reads as a step: "Step 2 · About you", then how many questions it holds. A header's ⋯ has Rename (in place), Add logic (one click to a ready rule: When <its choice question> is <its first option>, Jump to <the next step>, or to an ending made for it), Show only if… and Merge with the step above (with Undo; merges made while the last one's toast still shows are said once, "Merged 2 steps into the ones above.", whose one Undo puts them all back, forms fixes 3). A step's rules sit under its last question (critique c, 7: read after the questions they read), headed After this step, each "If … → …" with "Otherwise → Step 3" under them and + Add a rule; its Show only if stays under its header. The preview shows the page you pressed or are editing, with Step 1 of 3 and Next. A rule still being written is kept as a local draft and saves once it has its value. With One at a time each question is a screen of its own and carries its own rules (`question.jumps`, AC-6313): the question's ⋯ has Add logic (one click to a ready rule reading it, past the next question to the one after, or to a thank-you message, made if need be), and the rules sit right under the question as "After this question", each "If … → Budget" with "Otherwise → …" under them and + Add a rule; only the questions below it are offered (`questionJumpChoices`), and a renamed question's rules follow its new key. Publish refuses an empty step, a step's Show when over a later question, and a Show when on the step holding the email a submission is matched by.
- **Logic** (`logic.ts`; T41, 5 November 2026: "the rules need to be explicit and smart"): every rule reads as a sentence with a visible Otherwise ("If Budget is Under $1k → Thank-you message: Not a fit", "Otherwise → Step 3"; "Otherwise → this question is skipped" under a Show only if), values picked from the question's real choices, and a question's row carries a "Shown if …" chip. `logicProblems` (pure, unit tested) finds a rule that can never match, one reading a question that is gone, an earlier rule that always wins (the first match wins, and the card says which), a step nobody reaches (with One at a time, a question nobody reaches), a jump back up the form (ignored, never a loop) and a required question some people never see; each is said as a warning on the rule itself. A renamed choice is renamed in every rule (`followChoices`); removing a choice, or deleting a question, that rules read takes those rules with it under one Undo, said in the toast. The Questions header's "Logic · N" (a warning mark and "N to check" when something needs a look) is a toggle (forms fixes 3, the design critic: the Logic map said only Edit and sent you elsewhere, so it went): pressed, the list shows only the questions and steps that carry a Show only if or rules of their own, nothing moving and nothing added meanwhile; off while the form has no rules. Every card says its rules on its second line, beside its Shown if chip: "Skips to Budget" for one, "3 rules" for more. `logicSuggestions` and `testPath` stay in `logic.ts`, unit tested, for the day they have a place again.
- **After they send** (`AfterSendSection.tsx`; T39, 5 November 2026, after Typeform's and Tally's logic in words): the end of the Form tab, read as sentences, under a plain heading with no header actions. Each rule is a Card holding one line, "If [Budget] [is] [Under $1k] → [A different thank-you message]" (`ConditionLines` from `../routing`, the Automations dialog's Sentence with inline Selects), its value control following the question (its real choices, yes or no, a number box, a short box, never "Set value…"), and the destination picked in the sentence: a different thank-you message (its heading, line and button under the line), a live booking page by name, or a link (its address under the line). The rule's ⋯ holds Add a condition, Move up, Move down (the first match wins) and Delete, with Undo. The section's head holds "+ Add a route", a secondary button (forms fixes 3, after Calendly's Routes card), which makes a ready rule on the first choice or yes or no question (never a consent, a name, an email or a phone; `ruleQuestion` in `logic.ts`), to a new thank-you message, and focuses its question. While a thank-you message's words have focus, the preview shows that message in the form's place (HostedFormPreview's `ending`); leaving them gives it back. Then one Card of labelled rows (forms fixes 3: the sentence read unfinished), titled Everyone else once a rule exists: Show (Thank you page, Booking page or Link), then Message; or Booking page and Countdown; or Address. The document keeps its shapes: `after`, `endings`, `routing.rules`, `scoreFiledOn`.

## Rules

- `draft.ts` holds every change to a draft as a plain function; `draft.test.ts` checks them.
- The publish check is `publishProblem` from `@crm/data/forms`, the server's own.
- "Submission" is the one word for what a form collects, in every string here; "answer" is one question's reply (Taking submissions, New submissions, Keep submissions, the Submissions figure; Save answers to, Answers per question).
- **The full page Preview** (`../../routes/w.$slug.captures_.forms.$formId_.preview.tsx`, lane 3c): the library's `PreviewPage`: a bar with Back to editor (it closes the tab the editor opened, else goes to the editor), the form's name and Desktop or Phone, then "Preview · nothing is sent", then the form in the height left, its foot in view.
- The visitor's page is `src/public/main.ts`, drawn by `@crm/ui/public-form` with React and the library's own controls, under its budget in `apps/web/.size-limit.js` (223 kB of JavaScript and 17 kB of CSS gzipped). Steps and One at a time are their own lazy chunk, at most 10 kB, which a Classic form never loads (spec 0063).
