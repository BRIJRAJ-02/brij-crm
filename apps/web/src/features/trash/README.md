# Trash

Spec 0022, milestones 1 to 4. The workspace's trash: a record deleted from a table waits here for 30 days, then the daily cleanup removes it for good.

## The trash (`/w/$slug/settings/trash`, TrashScreen)

- **Purpose**: every record deleted in the last 30 days that the person may see, newest first.
- **Main task**: find a record deleted by mistake and restore it, seeing on its row why one can't come back; owners and admins also delete forever and empty the trash.
- **Leaves out**: opening or editing trashed records, and changing the 30 days.

## Notes

- Trash is reached from Settings › Data › Trash, at /w/$slug/settings/trash; the old /w/$slug/trash redirects there, keeping `?object=`. It draws inside Settings (A120, 23 October 2026): the settings sidebar stays, Trash current in it, and the bar's trail reads Settings › Data › Trash. Since 5 November 2026 it is a settings Table, as Members' and History's are, in the settings column (the records grid filling the view was clipped there): the TopBar counts what the trash holds ("10,000+" past the cap), and shows no count while it is empty, since the empty state says so; the Kind select at the body's top (an inline Select: "All kinds", then every object the person may see, archived ones marked) narrows it, kept in the address as `?object=<id>`.
- The list is a Table fed by `data.trash.view` (windows by cursor, 100 a block, with the count), through `useTrash`: its first 100 rows, then Show more in the Table's foot beside "100 of 1,234 records" reads the next block (no foot once every row shows: the bar's badge says the count). Its columns: Name (inline, a refused restore's words beside it), Kind (the object's name for one record, "Person", an archived one marked), Deleted by, Deleted and Deleted forever on (the day the daily cleanup takes it, in the workspace's zone), each value but Kind through its one field design (the synthetic attributes in `trash.ts`: a record reference, an actor, a timestamp, a date), then the row's Restore and, for owners and admins, its ⋯ with Delete forever. A record of an object the person may only read says View only in place of Restore. On a phone each row reads as two lines (`phoneLayout="rows"`). A name hidden from the person reads "Unnamed <singular>", like an empty one.
- A row's Restore brings that record back; ticking rows shows the BulkActionBar at the view's foot (`PreviewFooter`, `placement="footer"`) with Restore for several, and its Clear gives focus to the title. A row's own Restore or Delete forever (whose confirm names the record) leaves the ticks alone. It is enabled when at least one selected record's object is one the person may change. Restored rows leave at once; a refused one stays with "Not restored" (a red dot) and an InfoTip with its reason (the view's `cellErrors`, kept until the person leaves), and one toast sums it up ("Restored 46 records. 2 couldn’t be restored; each row says why."). Focus goes to the page's title, since the control went with its row.
- States: the Table's loading rows under its columns (`TrashPending`), "The trash is empty. Deleted records stay here for 30 days." and "No deleted <plural>." for an object with none, each an `EmptyState layout="block"`, and a block error with Retry.
- Live: deletes, restores and purges anywhere settle the open trash 1.5 seconds after the last (spread 0 to 2 seconds). This tab's own deletes and undos settle it too, since their echoes never come back.
- More than 500, or "Select all N matching" (the trash of the object filter, less any left out), restore as a background change: counted ("1,200 records counted", with Review), confirmed ("Restore 1,200 records?"), with progress and Cancel in the bar. A restore or Delete forever that refuses some keeps those rows ticked, each with its reason on its row.
- Owners and admins (`records.purge`) see Delete forever in the bar (a danger confirm, "Delete 48 records forever? This can't be undone.", inline up to 500, a counted job past that) and Empty trash in the TopBar (a quiet ghost button, the danger in its confirm; always a counted job: "Empty the trash? 1,234 records will be deleted forever.", or "Empty deleted deals", "Delete all deals in the trash forever?" and "12 deals will be deleted forever." while the filter names one object). Empty trash hands focus to the bar's Cancel as its job starts, says its refusal or a count of zero in a toast, and is off, saying why, while a change runs. Delete forever fails closed: a record of an object the person may only read stays, with its reason. Members never see either; the server refuses them 403 anyway.
