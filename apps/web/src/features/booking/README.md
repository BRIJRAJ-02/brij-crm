# Booking pages (spec 0025)

## Captures › Links (`/w/$slug/captures/links`; the old `/settings/booking-pages` redirects here)

- Purpose: the member's booking pages (an admin sees everyone's) with their status and bookings this month (AC-645).
- Main task: make a page and open its builder; copy a link, pause or take bookings again, delete one after a confirm.
- Leaves out: a page's settings (its builder), the bookings list (milestone 2) and team pages (milestone 3).

Built from Table, Badge, CopyButton, Button, Modal (new page, and the delete confirm with `tone="danger"`: Delete page stays busy until the server answers while the card has already left, a refusal shows inside it with the card back, and once deleted focus goes to the page's title), Field, Callout and EmptyState. Pause shows at once and rolls back on a refusal.

## The builder (`/w/$slug/captures/links/$pageId`; the old `/settings/booking-pages/$pageId` redirects here)

- Purpose: one page's details, meeting lengths, location, availability and limits (AC-623, AC-624).
- Main task: change a setting and have it saved half a second after the last change; what failed is said where it happened.
- Leaves out: questions and the deal step (milestone 2), team members (milestone 3), and the order of pages on the profile (they keep the order they were made in).

Cards of Switch, Field, Checkbox, Select and DatePicker. The Hours card picks the schedule the page books by (spec 0035, AC-965): the default schedule by its name with a Default badge, which follows whichever schedule is the owner's default at each slot read, or one of the owner's schedules by name, then edits that schedule's week and days off in place (below). The owner's schedules come from the data layer's held list (`data.schedules.view`), so a save elsewhere shows in the card. While the reader has no calendar connected (`data.calendar.view`, read through `useCalendar`), a Callout says the page offers their hours minus its bookings (AC-631), with a link to Settings, Calendar when sync is on here. The editor sits in PreviewLayout, the one Captures editor layout it shares with the form editor: notices above, then a Tabs strip, one tab per group (Page, Meeting, Hosts on a round robin page, Hours, Limits; the owner, 22 October 2026: the stacked cards were too much to look through). Each tab's headed sections, each a Card, fill the first two thirds of the width (the owner, 23 October 2026: "full width the left"; before that `bodyWidth="form"` held them to a centred column), beside a sticky live preview of the public page in a phone centred in the last third (`PreviewFrame` on `/b/:key`, posting the unsaved title, description, lengths and location, and loading again after a save of the page or of the schedule it books by). Under 992px of layout the phone waits behind a Preview button that opens it in a sheet. The owner, 22 October 2026: the masonry columns that replaced the preview split the tabs badly, so the preview came back, phone only. The save bar sits in the layout's `footer`. The Page tab has three sections: Details (Description, with a placeholder), Sharing (one Address row: with a handle and a Public page, the full address with the part after the handle editable inline, then Copy and Open on the saved link; otherwise the read only /b/ link with Copy and Open, plus "Set your handle to get a short address" linking to Settings › Profile when there is no handle; then Who can book, Public or Link only, with one line for Link only), and Profile for a Public page only (Section, a Select of the owner's sections from their other pages that also creates a new one, or No section). The profile's order has no field: pages keep the order they were made in (a drag order on the Links list is left for later). The Meeting tab has Meeting (Title, lengths), Location and Calendar (Add bookings to). The Hours tab reads Schedule (with Manage schedules at its head), Weekly hours (the zone at its head), Days off, Reserved time on a link only page, then Conflicts. The Limits tab has one Card per group (Buffers, Booking window, Pace), each setting a SettingsRow with its InfoTip. The tab is in `?tab=` (`pageEditorHref(slug, id, tab)`, `PAGE_TABS` in `links.ts`), replaced rather than pushed, and the loader doesn't rerun on a tab change. A tab whose fields hold an error, or which a refusal names, carries the Tabs `alert` dot. While the page loads (`PageEditorPending`), the chosen tab's sections show titled and busy where they will stand: Profile and Reserved time wait for the page, which says whether it has them, and Hours draws a one on one page's.

Who can book (Page tab, Sharing) is Public or Link only, one SegmentedControl in a FieldGroup, with one line under it for Link only. Public books inside the hours and limits and is listed on the owner's profile (`/<handle>`), and Link only (`isPrivate`, one combined flag) also books reserved time and ignores the minimum notice; the lists tag a link only page "Link only" and public pages carry no tag. Bookings' "Create a link only page" opens `newLinkOnlyPageHref` (Links with `?new=link-only`), where New booking page opens preset to Link only and lands in its editor. The header is the Captures editors' shared one (`../captures/EditorActions.tsx`): Live or Paused beside the name, then Copy link and a ⋯ menu with Pause or Resume (at once, a toast with Undo), Duplicate, and Delete booking page (`DeletePageDialog.tsx`, shared with Links).

The Meeting tab ends with Add bookings to (`PageDestination.tsx`, spec 0024 AC-997): for the page's owner, Default (naming it, its note a muted line under it in the list) then each calendar in use they can write (`isCalendarInUse`; the page's own pick stays listed even when it isn't in use, until the page moves off it), built by `calendarOptions` (`../calendars/calendar-options.ts`, shared with Settings › Calendars): flat for an account with one, under its address for an account with two or more, each dot in the calendar's own colour, saved at once like the Live switch; an error when the chosen calendar can't take bookings now (they go to the default); a colleague reads it only, never a name (spec 0037). The Hours tab (the owner, 22 October 2026: keep the availability editor here, like Calendly's and Cal.com's event Availability tab) holds the Schedule select (a Card with Manage schedules to Availability at its head, and, when other pages book by the same schedule, "3 pages use Working hours. Changes here apply to all of them." under it; it waits while the hours have unsaved changes), then the chosen schedule's week (`WeekCard` from `ScheduleEditor.tsx`, the same card as Availability's, the zone at its head) and its Days off, edited in place through `useScheduleDraft` and saved by the page's own Save bar with the page's settings (Discard puts both back). While the schedules load the week's card shows in its loading shape (`WeekCardPending`); when they can't be read, the week the page books by shows read only (`weekGroups`, `zoneDisplay`) with a line saying why. Then Reserved time on a link only page, then Conflicts (`PageConflicts`, a Card: the calendars that hold the page's times back as Badges in their own colour, from `calendarOptions`, with Manage to Settings › Calendars, or No calendars connected with Connect calendar). The Limits tab shows every setting as SettingsRows in one Card per group (Buffers, Booking window, Pace), each with an InfoTip saying what it does with an example; nothing folds away (the owner, 22 October 2026: show all upfront).

## Availability (`/w/$slug/bookings/availability`, spec 0035)

- Purpose: the open schedule's editor, the Calendly way (22 October 2026): the default opens first, `?schedule=<id>` opens another (AC-969, AC-970).
- Main task: change the weekly hours (add or remove a day's ranges, copy them to other days), the time zone or the days off, each saved as it changes; switch, make, rename, set as default, duplicate or delete a schedule from the switcher.
- Leaves out: a list of schedules as rows (the switcher's menu lists them), which schedule a page uses (each page picks in its own editor) and a calendar view of a schedule.

The tab strip's end holds the switcher, as the other tabs' tools sit there (the owner, 22 October 2026: no row of its own floating over the card) (`ScheduleSwitcher.tsx`: the open schedule's name as a menu button, Default beside it; the menu hugs its content: the schedules to switch to (the open one checked, Default tagged), New schedule, then this schedule's Rename, Duplicate, Set as default and Delete, with no item descriptions; an action that can't apply is left out, not disabled (no Set as default on the default, no Delete on the default or the only schedule, nothing while unsaved), and the Schedules and This schedule headings show only with two or more schedules, the owner's ask of 22 October 2026. The week (HoursEditor) is a card with the time zone Select at its head and, beside it, the days off (DaysOff) and Used by (the pages booking by it, each a row opening its editor). Both side cards share one anatomy (the owner, 22 October 2026): the title, then rows, or one quiet EmptyState row ("No days off yet", "No booking pages use this schedule"); Days off then has Add day off under its rows, and each day off row ends in Remove. New schedule stays the TopBar's primary too; at 20 schedules both wait and say why. The old `/bookings/availability/$scheduleId` address redirects into the tab.

Saving is explicit (the owner, 22 October 2026, replacing the save as you go rule of 21 October): the hours, the zone and the days off wait on screen (`useScheduleDraft` in `scheduleDraft.ts`) until Save in the library's Unsaved changes bar (⌘S), as in the booking page editor; Discard puts the saved hours back, and leaving with changes asks first. A landed save says "Saved <name>." in a toast; a failure keeps the changes with the reason in the bar; one beaten by another tab or person (`STALE`) shows theirs and says so. The public page reads its times again when it is shown again (`visibilitychange`). A range that doesn't read yet waits on screen, unsaved, with the editor's own error. The name is a text field, so Rename asks in a Modal with its own button. The schedules come from the data layer's held list (`data.schedules.view`), so another tab's save shows within a second. Until the member saves any hours, the list holds the virtual default, "Working hours", weekdays 9 to 5 in the browser's zone (AC-963), which opens like any other and is stored by its first change.

An admin (`booking.admin`) has a Viewing Select at the strip's end once the workspace has a colleague to pick (or while a colleague's schedules are open, so the way back stays; `showsViewing`; 22 October 2026: alone, it stood beside the switcher as a second menu with nothing in it, and Cal.com shows its member switcher only on a team): their own schedules or a colleague's by name (`?member=`, AC-971), the choices from one service (`data.schedules.members`). A colleague's confirms and toasts say whose they are ("their default"); a day off's note stays its member's alone, so an admin sees the days without the reasons, and the server keeps the reasons through an admin's save.

Deleting asks first (the owner's confirm rule, 21 October 2026): "Delete the Evenings schedule?", one sentence naming the pages it moves to the default and that it can't be undone, Cancel (Esc) then Delete schedule. The dialog waits for the server and says a refusal inside itself; once it's gone the tab opens the default. The default can't be deleted: its menu item says to set another first.

The page editor's Hours card picks the default schedule (its name with a Default badge, `scheduleChoices` in `schedules.ts`) or one of the owner's schedules, and edits its week and days off in place. The copy is in `schedule-strings.ts`, apart from `strings.ts`, so it never loads with the route loaders.

## New booking page (22 Oct 2026)

`NewPageDialog`: Title, Duration (a compact SegmentedControl with a visible label: 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or Custom, which shows a Minutes field: 5 to 480 in steps of 5, the same bound as the contract and the database check from migration 0062) and Schedule (the same `scheduleChoices` as the editor, one sentence InfoTip), then Cancel and Create page. No web address field: the server makes a free slug from the title, and no visitor sees it since links use the page's key, so the editor drops its Short name field too. The page takes one length here; more are added in the editor, whose Durations chips show the ready picks plus the page's own custom lengths, with a "+ Custom" chip at their end (ChoiceChips `end`) that opens a Minutes field in a Popover (at most four lengths, as the public page shows four; the contract refuses a fifth, `durations.ts`).
