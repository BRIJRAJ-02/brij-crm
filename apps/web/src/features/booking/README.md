# Booking pages (spec 0025)

## Captures › Links (`/w/$slug/captures/links`; the old `/settings/booking-pages` redirects here)

- Purpose: the member's booking pages (an admin sees everyone's) with their status and bookings this month (AC-645).
- Main task: make a page and open its builder; copy a link, pause or take bookings again, delete one after a confirm.
- Leaves out: a page's settings (its builder), the bookings list (milestone 2) and team pages (milestone 3).

Built from Table, Badge, CopyButton, Button, Modal (new page, and the delete confirm with `tone="danger"`: Delete page stays busy until the server answers while the card has already left, a refusal shows inside it with the card back, and once deleted focus goes to the page's title), Field, Callout and EmptyState. Pause shows at once and rolls back on a refusal.

## The builder (`/w/$slug/captures/links/$pageId`; the old `/settings/booking-pages/$pageId` redirects here)

- Purpose: one page's details, meeting lengths, location, availability and limits (AC-623, AC-624).
- Main task: change a setting and have it saved half a second after the last change; what failed is said where it happened.
- Leaves out: questions and the deal step (milestone 2), team members (milestone 3), and the order of pages on the profile (Settings › Profile arranges it).

Cards of Switch, Field, Checkbox, Select and DatePicker. The Hours card picks the schedule the page books by (spec 0035, AC-965): the default schedule by its name with a Default badge, which follows whichever schedule is the owner's default at each slot read, or one of the owner's schedules by name, then edits that schedule's week and days off in place (below). The owner's schedules come from the data layer's held list (`data.schedules.view`), so a save elsewhere shows in the card. While the reader has no calendar connected (`data.calendar.view`, read through `useCalendar`), a Callout says the page offers their hours minus its bookings (AC-631), with a link to Settings, Calendar when sync is on here. The editor sits in PreviewLayout, the one Captures editor layout it shares with the form editor: notices above, then a Tabs strip, one tab per group (Page, Meeting, Hosts on a round robin page, Hours, Limits; the owner, 22 October 2026: the stacked cards were too much to look through). Each tab's headed sections, each a Card, fill the first two thirds of the width (the owner, 23 October 2026: "full width the left"; before that `bodyWidth="form"` held them to a centred column), beside a sticky live preview of the public page in a phone centred in the last third (`PreviewFrame` on `/b/:key`, posting the unsaved title, description, lengths, location and opening view, and loading again after a save of the page or of the schedule it books by). Under 992px of layout the phone waits behind a Preview button that opens it in a sheet. The owner, 22 October 2026: the masonry columns that replaced the preview split the tabs badly, so the preview came back, phone only. The save bar sits in the layout's `footer`, with `LeaveGuard`: leaving with unsaved changes asks first in a Modal, while moving between the tabs (`?tab=`) keeps the editor and its changes and never asks. The Page tab has three sections: Details (Description, with a placeholder, then Opens in: a FieldGroup holding a horizontal RadioGroup of Month, List and Book by chat, the view the page opens in for a visitor who hasn't chosen one of their own, the owner, 25 October 2026), Sharing (one Address row: with a handle and a Public page, the full address with the part after the handle editable inline, then Copy and Open on the saved link; otherwise the read only /b/ link with Copy and Open, plus "Set your handle to get a short address" linking to Settings › Profile when there is no handle; then Who can book, Public or Link only, with one line for Link only), and Link page for a Public page only, shown to its owner (On your link page, with Edit link page: a Public page joins the end of the owner's link page by itself, and its place, heading or archive are set there, spec 0051). ## Availability (`/w/$slug/bookings/availability`, spec 0035)

- Purpose: the open schedule's editor, the Calendly way (22 October 2026): the default opens first, `?schedule=<id>` opens another (AC-969, AC-970).
- Main task: change the weekly hours (add or remove a day's ranges, copy them to other days), the time zone or the days off, each saved as it changes; switch, make, rename, set as default, duplicate or delete a schedule from the switcher.
- Leaves out: a list of schedules as rows (the switcher's menu lists them), which schedule a page uses (each page picks in its own editor) and a calendar view of a schedule.

The tab strip's end holds the switcher, as the other tabs' tools sit there (the owner, 22 October 2026: no row of its own floating over the card) (`ScheduleSwitcher.tsx`: the open schedule's name as a menu button, Default beside it; the menu hugs its content: the schedules to switch to (the open one checked, Default tagged), New schedule, then this schedule's Rename, Duplicate, Set as default and Delete, with no item descriptions; an action that can't apply is left out, not disabled (no Set as default on the default, no Delete on the default or the only schedule, nothing while unsaved), and the Schedules and This schedule headings show only with two or more schedules, the owner's ask of 22 October 2026. The week (HoursEditor) is a card with the time zone Select at its head and, beside it, the days off (DaysOff) and Used by (the pages booking by it, each a row opening its editor). Both side cards share one anatomy (the owner, 22 October 2026): the title, then rows, or one quiet EmptyState row ("No days off yet", "No booking pages use this schedule"); Days off then has Add day off under its rows, and each day off row ends in Remove. New schedule stays the TopBar's primary too; at 20 schedules both wait and say why. The old `/bookings/availability/$scheduleId` address redirects into the tab.

Saving is explicit (the owner, 22 October 2026, replacing the save as you go rule of 21 October): the hours, the zone and the days off wait on screen (`useScheduleDraft` in `scheduleDraft.ts`) until Save in the library's Unsaved changes bar (⌘S), as in the booking page editor; Discard puts the saved hours back, and leaving with changes asks first, in a danger Modal (`LeaveGuard.tsx`: "Leave without saving?", Keep editing (Esc) or Leave without saving; another schedule counts as leaving; closing the tab asks the browser's way). A landed save says "Saved <name>." in a toast; a failure keeps the changes with the reason in the bar; one beaten by another tab or person (`STALE`) shows theirs and says so. The public page reads its times again when it is shown again (`visibilitychange`). A range that doesn't read yet waits on screen, unsaved, with the editor's own error. The name is a text field, so Rename asks in a Modal with its own button. The schedules come from the data layer's held list (`data.schedules.view`), so another tab's save shows within a second. Until the member saves any hours, the list holds the virtual default, "Working hours", weekdays 9 to 5 in the browser's zone (AC-963), which opens like any other and is stored by its first change.

Anyone who may see a colleague's scheduling has a Viewing Select at the strip's end once the workspace has a colleague to pick (or while a colleague's schedules are open, so the way back stays; `showsViewing`; 22 October 2026: alone, it stood beside the switcher as a second menu with nothing in it, and Cal.com shows its member switcher only on a team): their own schedules or a colleague's by name (`?member=`, AC-971), the choices from the scheduling reach (the scheduling store's `visible`, opened by the loader with `data.scheduling.open()`, spec 0037, AC-1268: the reader first, a colleague they may only See marked See only and shown read only with "You can see Bea's hours but not change them.", one they Manage with "Bea will be told about changes."; one's own hours without `availability.own` read only too, AC-1260; "Changed by Priya, 2 hours ago" when someone else changed it last, AC-1265). A colleague's confirms and toasts say whose they are ("their default"); a day off's note stays its member's alone, so an admin sees the days without the reasons, and the server keeps the reasons through an admin's save.

Deleting asks first (the owner's confirm rule, 21 October 2026): "Delete the Evenings schedule?", one sentence naming the pages it moves to the default and that it can't be undone, Cancel (Esc) then Delete schedule. The dialog waits for the server and says a refusal inside itself; once it's gone the tab opens the default. The default can't be deleted: its menu item says to set another first.

The page editor's Hours card picks the default schedule (its name with a Default badge, `scheduleChoices` in `schedules.ts`) or one of the owner's schedules, and edits its week and days off in place. The copy is in `schedule-strings.ts`, apart from `strings.ts`, so it never loads with the route loaders.

## New booking page (22 Oct 2026)

`NewPageDialog`: Title, Duration (a compact SegmentedControl with a visible label: 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or Custom, which shows a Minutes field: 5 to 480 in steps of 5, the same bound as the contract and the database check from migration 0062) and Schedule (the same `scheduleChoices` as the editor, one sentence InfoTip), then Cancel and Create page. ## Settings › Scheduling › Round robin (`/w/$slug/settings/scheduling/round-robin`, RotationsScreen; `/settings/scheduling/rotations` redirects here)

- Purpose: the groups of teammates round robin pages share meetings across (spec 0029; rotations in code), and each one's fair share.
- Main task: an admin (`booking.admin`) makes a group and changes or deletes one; anyone who makes booking pages (`booking.manage`) sees the groups they are in and their own share. Without `booking.manage` the page isn't there: the route reads the reader's access first (cached) and shows Page not found, reading nothing else (SS-R10).
- Leaves out: editing in place, a group's history, and marking in the picker who can't join (that needs the server to say who can host).

New group shows once (SS-R2): while there are no groups it is the empty state's one primary (or, alone in the workspace, Invite teammates is), and once a group exists it is the bar's. "How round robin works" sits beside the title as an InfoTip, three steps and a Create a booking page link. Each group is a Card whose line reads "Fair · last 30 days · 1 booking page" ("not on a booking page yet" with none), with Members and Distribution tabs; a share a member may not see reads "Only admins see this". Distribution says what its numbers are as the Share of meetings section's line, "Meetings in the last 30 days, against each person’s fair share." (a member, who sees only their own row and no fair share, reads "Your meetings in the last 30 days."), not a Callout (SS-R9).

The group dialog (`RotationDialog`): Name (its error goes as soon as a name is typed, SS-R3), How it picks, "Count meetings from the last" with an InfoTip, Returning guests go to, and Add a member, whose hint reads "2 of 20 members. Only people with hours or a calendar can join." (SS-R1), as plain text: a link there would open the reader's own hours and leave the half made group. Any refusal shows in a danger Callout at the top. One about a member, taken from the list that was sent (so a member removed above them since doesn't shift it), offers Remove <name> from the group, and, while they are an active member (so hours are what they lack; "Only active members…" gets the remove alone), Set hours, a ButtonLink to that member's hours (`availabilityHref(slug, memberId)`) in a new tab so the group stays. Removing that member clears the refusal. A distinct refusal code from the server would replace the active member check.
