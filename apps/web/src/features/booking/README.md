# Booking pages (spec 0025)

## Bookings › Pages and Captures › Links (`/w/$slug/bookings/pages`, `/w/$slug/captures/links`; the old `/settings/booking-pages` redirects here)

- Purpose: the member's booking pages as grouped cards (Your pages, Workspace, Members' pages, Managed pages; an admin sees everyone's), each with its facts, hosts and link (AC-645, the owner's mock of 9 November 2026).
- Main task: make a page and open its editor; copy a link, make a page Live or Hidden from its pill (at once, with Undo), or delete one with Undo (no confirm: the card leaves and the page is deleted on the server after ten seconds, or when the screen is left).
- Leaves out: a page's settings (its builder), the bookings list (milestone 2) and team pages (milestone 3).

Built from Table, Badge, CopyButton, Button, Modal (new page), Field, Callout and EmptyState. Delete, from a card or the builder's ⋯ (which goes back to Pages), takes the page out of every list at once and shows a toast with Undo for ten seconds (`delete-page.ts`, through `bookingPages.removeLater`, whose wait outlasts the screen); only then is it deleted on the server, a refusal brings it back and says why, and focus goes to the page's title (or, on Undo, to the card that came back).

Each card's Live or Hidden menu is `PageStatusMenu.tsx`; its facts line comes from `page-card.ts`. Live or Hidden shows at once and rolls back on a refusal.

## Managed pages (ManagedPageEditor.tsx, spec 0037 L10, AC-1333 to AC-1336)

- Purpose: one booking page an admin sets up, with a page of it for each person on it, and the parts those people can't change.
- Main task: name it, switch on the parts the workspace sets, pick who has it, and delete it when it has served its purpose.
- Leaves out: the member pages themselves (each opens in the ordinary page editor, where the locked parts are read only), and following a team, which the server refuses until the hook that keeps it in step exists.

It is reached from Captures › Links, where the templates sit in a **Managed pages** group above the bookable pages, and made from New › "Everyone, their own page" (`booking.admin`), which makes the template in one step and lands in this editor, as New › Team link does with a form. A template is not a bookable page, so its cards have no switch, no Copy link and no Open: only its editor and the one ending that is its own.

Details and the lock Switches save together through the SaveBar (in `PageColumn`'s `footer`, so it stays in view), because that one save is what moves every member page (AC-1334): the toast says how many it moved ("Saved, and all 12 pages made from this were updated too."), since a bare "Saved" hides the point of a locked part. The people save as each change is made instead, because adding or taking someone off makes or deletes a whole page and neither belongs in a batch with a title change.

**The two save models have one rule between them**: while the details or locks are unsaved, the people picker and each row's x wait, with "Save your changes first." Without it an admin could switch on two locks, add Kim from the card directly below, and give Kim a page built from the settings as last saved — with a success toast saying nothing was wrong. The card's line says the asymmetry out loud rather than leaving it to be discovered. The bar's status is derived from `isDirty`, never latched: a latched "saved" once left Save dead after the first save of a session.

**The two endings are opposites, and the outcome is in the button on both** — because a hurried admin reads the title and the red button, not the paragraph. Taking someone off _deletes_ their page: "Take Sam Lee off this managed page?" / **Delete Sam Lee's page**. Deleting the template _keeps_ every page: "Stop managing the Intro call pages?" / **Stop managing, keep the pages**. Out of context neither button can be read as the other, which is the test. Both bodies are one line, the dismiss on each reads **Cancel** (never "Keep it", which an admin skimming for "do my people keep their pages?" reads as a promise), and the delete body says "no page is affected" rather than "nothing is deleted" — the template itself does go, so the broader claim would be false. The count branches at 0, 1 and 12 are written out rather than patched with plurals. The second question is asked in both places a template can be deleted: this editor and the card's menu in Links.

The Hours rule reads differently from the other parts on purpose: it is a rule, not a value ("Everyone books on their own default working hours, and can't point their page at another schedule"), because a member page always books on its own member's hours (AC-1336) and the template's schedule belongs to someone else.

On the **member's** side there is no screen of its own: their page opens in `PageEditor`, which takes `templateId` and `lockedParts` off `BookingPageView`. A "Set by your workspace" Badge sits beside the page's status, and one Callout above the tabs names the parts ("Your workspace sets the title and description, the lengths and the limits on this page. Everything else is yours to change."), rather than repeating a note on every field. Each of those controls is `isReadOnly`, and the server refuses a write to one whatever this renders (`lockedPartOfWrite`), so the note is a label over a guard, never the guard.
