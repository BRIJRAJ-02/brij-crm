# The reservation page (spec 0066 B7 lite)

`/r/<key>` and `/r/<key>/manage` on the public origin (timefix.com), loaded from `reserve.html` so a guest never
downloads the app. Built on `@crm/ui/public-booking`, as the booking page is.

| File                    | Brief                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `ReservePage.tsx` | In checklabs' order and words on the booking page's frame, in the guest menu's look (GuestLook, in `main.tsx`, wearing `look.css` when the facts name a version). Month view, "Choose a date": PartyBand, the date (MonthTiles `plain`; on a phone a DayStrip of three weeks with Full calendar a press away, closing on a pick), one grouped SlotList (Lunch and Dinner), and the Book bar ("Pick a time", then "Continue · {day}, {time}"; on a phone only once a time is picked, brought into view, never sticky). Details step over the whole card with Back: Full name, Phone, Email, Special requests, Seating when the place has areas (sent at the head of the note, as checklabs sends it), Occasion, the menu link, news and offers unticked, "Confirm {day} at {time}". What is typed survives Back and a time taken meanwhile (BookingDetails' draft). A time taken comes back with the next free times beside the reason, and the day read again. |
| `ManageReservation.tsx` | The guest's reservation (BookingReceipt): by the link's token (in the fragment) or by the code with the email; Add to calendar, change time and party (BookingChanges' Move with the page's own picker) and cancel within the cutoffs, else the restaurant's phone.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `NewsConfirm.tsx` | The news and offers email's link (double opt-in), `/r/<key>/manage#news=<token>`: "Get news from {business}?" and one Yes, sent only on the press (link scanners open links), then "You’re signed up" (or already), or "This link no longer works". The token leaves the address bar once read. |
| `days.ts`               | The pure helpers: open days, Lunch and Dinner, labels on the place's clock.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `main.tsx`              | The entry: the provider, the embed's height, and the move to the manage address once reserved.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
